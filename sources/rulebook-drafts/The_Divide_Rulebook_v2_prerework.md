# **THE DIVIDE: Rulebook**

*A game of divine ambition, hidden loyalties, and the line where creation hangs in the balance.*

**Created by:** Robert Treviño
**Players:** 4 to 6 | **Play Time:** 45 to 60 minutes
**Mechanisms:** Hidden Allegiance, Social Deduction, Variable Player Powers, Tableau Building, Negotiation, Tug-of-War

---

## **What This Game Is**

Before the first dawn, the unfinished world rests on the Divide, the narrow span between the Heights above and the Abyss below. You are a Celestial, a primordial power with a secret agenda for where the world should fall. Over six Days, you and your rivals will push it toward the light, drag it toward the dark, or fight to hold it exactly where it lies.

**To win, you need two things:**

1. When the game ends, the world must belong to your secret faction.
2. Among the players of that faction, you must hold the most Glory.

Faction victory makes you eligible. Glory makes you the winner. Neither is enough on its own, and that gap is where the game lives: you must help your side win the world while quietly out-earning the very allies you are helping.

---

## **The Five Things You Touch**

Everything in The Divide runs through five objects. Learn these and the rest of the rules are just procedure.

**The Balance.** An 11-space track in the center of the table with a single marker on it. The middle 3 spaces are the Divide. The 4 spaces on each end are the Heights (Light) and the Abyss (Dark). The marker's final position decides which faction controls the world.

**The Altar.** A shared pool of cards in the center of the table. Cards played here are public, and at the end of each Day their combined Light and Darkness decide which way the Balance moves. The Altar is emptied every night. Nothing on it is safe or permanent: cards placed there can be taken by anyone before the Day ends.

**Your Choir.** Your personal row of up to 3 face-up cards. This is your private engine. Your Choir's cards give you abilities, and each night the Choir is scored: lean the same way the world moved and you profit, lean against it and you pay.

**Your Hand.** Three hidden Host cards, refilled each night. Your hand is the only hidden zone you control, and every card you play from it tells the table something.

**Glory.** Points and money in one. You earn it by playing cards and scoring your Choir, you spend it to build and to defect, and you may hand it to other players to seal a deal. Glory never drops below 0. The most Glory within the winning faction takes the game.

### **Reading a Host Card**

The Host deck is the game's one deck of playable cards: 66 in total, split evenly into 22 Light, 22 Dark, and 22 Neutral or Variable. Every Host card shows:

* A **name** and an **ability**, with a cadence telling you when the ability triggers (on your turn, passively, once per game, and so on).
* An **Offering value**: Light +1 or +2, Dark +1 or +2, or Neutral. A card's Offering pushes the Balance while on the Altar and shapes your Choir's lean while in your Choir. Neutral cards push nothing and lean nowhere, which is sometimes exactly what you want. A +2 Offering appears only on once-per-game cards: the deck holds exactly ten, five Light and five Dark, and a spent once-per-game card keeps its +2 value (see Used Card in the Glossary).

---

## **How a Day Works**

The game lasts up to 6 rounds, called **Days**. Each morning an Event card changes the rules for that Day, one card is revealed onto the Altar to start the pool, and then every player takes exactly one turn, either feeding the Altar for Glory or building their own Choir. Each evening, the Altar is tallied: whichever side weighs more is the **Tide**, and the Balance marker moves that way. Then every Choir is scored against the Tide, the Altar is swept clean, hands refill, and a new Day dawns.

That is the whole loop. Push the world in public, position yourself in private, get paid when the two line up.

---

## **The Heart of the Game**

Your secret Fate wants the Balance in one place. Your Glory wants your Choir aligned with wherever the Tide is actually going. These two desires constantly split apart.

A Fallen player might spend a Day offering Light to the Altar because their Choir leans Light and they need the points. A Faithful player might sandbag a round to avoid tipping their hand. The Forsaken profits most when everyone else cancels each other out. Every card you play is both a move and a message, and the players who win are the ones who make the table read the message wrong.

---

## **Components**

* **The Balance:** an 11-space tug-of-war track (3 Divide spaces flanked by 4 Heights and 4 Abyss spaces), with 1 tracking marker.
* **66 Host cards:** the main deck. 22 Light, 22 Dark, 22 Neutral or Variable.
* **9 Celestial cards:** unique player powers.
* **20 Event cards:** one drawn each Day, changing that Day's rules.
* **Fate tokens:** secret allegiance markers (Faithful, Fallen, Forsaken).
* **Turn cards:** permanent defection markers kept beside the Altar.
* **Glory markers** and **1 starting player marker.**

---

## **Setup**

1. **Draw secret Fates.** Pool Fate tokens by player count using the table below, mix them in the bag, and have each player secretly draw one. Leave the rest in the bag, unrevealed. Because leftover tokens stay hidden, no one knows the true headcount of any faction. There may not even be a Forsaken at the table.

| Player Count | Faithful | Fallen | Forsaken | Left in Bag |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 4 Players | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 5 Players | 3 | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| 6 Players | 3 | 3 | 1 | 1 |

2. **Place the Balance** in the center with the marker on the middle space of the Divide.
3. **Set out the Turn cards** face up beside the Altar space: 1 card for 4 players, 2 cards for 5 or 6 players. The Altar itself starts empty.
4. **Deal Celestials.** Each player receives a Celestial card, face up. These asymmetric powers are public knowledge.
5. **Deal Host cards.** Each player takes a hand of 3, then deals 1 more Host card face up in front of them, free of cost, as the first card of their Choir.
6. **Finish.** Shuffle the Event deck, set all Glory to 0, and give a random player the starting player marker.

---

## **Morning Phase**

Every Morning opens with **the Unveiling**, a fixed three-step sequence resolved before anyone takes a turn:

1. **The Event.** If the Oracle is in play, they may first peek at the top Event card and choose to play it or bury it. The first player then draws the top Event card, reads it aloud, and places it face up. It changes the rules for this entire Day and applies to everyone equally.
2. **Declarations.** Any required predictions are locked in now. The Whisper makes their public call, and if Casting Lots is the Event, every player commits a secret prediction. All declarations happen before the Seed is revealed.
3. **The Seed.** Flip the top card of the Host deck onto the Altar. This is the Seed, the round's starting offering. (The Creation event reveals 3 Seeds; Pandemonium places the Seed facedown.)

Then, starting with the first player and moving clockwise, **each player takes exactly one turn.**

### **Your Turn: Choose One of Four Actions**

**Offer.** Play 1 Host card from your hand onto the Altar and immediately gain 1 Glory.
*Why: this is your income and your vote. The card's Offering now pulls the Tide, but the Glory is yours to keep even if the card is later taken. Remember that an Offer is provisional: anyone after you can Gather it away before the Day ends.*

**Gather.** Take 1 Host card from the Altar, or from the top of the Host deck, and add it directly to your Choir. If your Choir is full, you may discard one of its cards and pay 1 Glory to make room.
*Why: this is how you build for free, and how you play defense. You may take any card on the Altar, including the Seed or a card an opponent just offered. Stealing a Dark card off the Altar both strengthens your Choir and weakens the Dark Tide in a single action.*

**Exalt.** Pay 1 Glory to play a Host card from your hand into your Choir. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards and pay 1 additional Glory to replace it.
*Why: this is the only way to get a card from your hidden hand into your Choir without it ever touching the public Altar. You pay for the privacy.*

**Turn.** Once per game, defect to the opposing faction. This action has its own section below.

### **After Your Action: Activate Your Choir**

Once your action is complete, you may trigger the ability of each Host card in your Choir. Each card activates at most once per turn, and only in its printed cadence.

---

## **Evening Phase**

When every player has taken a turn, resolve the Evening in this exact order:

1. **Evening abilities.** Trigger any Celestial or Host abilities that reference the Evening.

2. **Set the Tide.** Total all Light values on the Altar, total all Darkness values, and compare. The heavier side is the Tide, and the Balance marker moves toward it: 1 space for a lead of exactly 1, or 2 spaces for a lead of 2 or more (2 is the cap). A tie, or an empty Altar, means a Neutral Tide and no movement.

   > *Example: the Altar holds Light +2, Light +1, Dark +1, Dark +1, and a Neutral card. Light totals 3, Darkness totals 2. The Tide is Light by a margin of 1, so the marker moves 1 space toward the Heights.*

3. **The Reaping.** Every player scores their Choir. Find your **Alignment**: the net difference between the Light and Darkness values in your Choir (Neutral cards count nothing).
   * If your Alignment matches the Tide's direction, **gain** Glory equal to your net value.
   * If it opposes the Tide, **lose** Glory equal to your net value (never below 0).
   * A net-zero Choir gains and loses nothing.
   * Alignment gains and losses are capped at 3: you never gain or lose more than 3 Glory from your Choir's Alignment at a single Reaping, even after doubling effects.

   > *Example: your Choir holds Free Will (Light +2), Confession (Light +1), and Moloch (Dark +1). Your net Alignment is Light 2. If the Tide is Light, you gain 2 Glory. If the Tide is Dark, you lose 2. Note that the margin of the Tide doesn't matter here, only its direction.*

4. **Refresh the Altar.** Discard every card remaining on the Altar. Nothing carries over.

5. **Upkeep.** Everyone draws back up to a hand of 3 (or discards down to 3), and the starting player marker passes left.

If the Balance marker has not ended the game (see Ending the Game), a new Day begins.

---

## **The Turn: Defection**

Once per game, during any Day except the last, a player may spend their action to **Turn**, defecting to the opposing faction. The Forsaken cannot Turn; they are bound to the center.

**To defect:** pay 1 Glory and place one of the face-up Turn cards from beside the Altar into an empty slot in your Choir. If your Choir is full, pay 1 additional Glory and discard a Choir card to clear the space.

**The Turn card is a scar, not a tool.** It is not a Host card. It has no Offering, no Alignment, and no ability. It cannot be Targeted, discarded, replaced, or counted by any effect that references Host cards, and it occupies its Choir slot for the rest of the game.

**Defection is not instant.** You remain your original Fate until the end of the current Day; your new Fate takes hold at the next Unveiling. A Turn taken on the final Day fails to resolve in time, and you score as your original Fate.

**Defections are scarce.** The supply of Turn cards is the limit: 1 total in 4-player games, 2 in games of 5 or 6.

---

## **Deals and Table Talk**

Open negotiation is permitted at all times, and the game expects you to use it.

* **Glory moves freely.** You may hand Glory to any player at any time, for any reason. Once given, it cannot be taken back.
* **Promises are worthless, which makes them valuable.** You may promise anything: future plays, alliances, safe passage. No promise is binding, and betrayal is a legal, legitimate strategy. Trust accordingly.
* **Cards never change hands by agreement.** Host cards, Fate tokens, and Turn cards can only move through explicit card actions and Celestial abilities. You cannot trade, gift, or sell them.

---

## **Ending the Game**

The game ends **immediately** if the Balance marker reaches the final space of the Heights or the Abyss. Otherwise it ends after the Evening of the 6th Day.

The marker's final position decides who controls the world:

* **The Heights:** the Faithful control the world.
* **The Abyss:** the Fallen control the world.
* **The Divide (center 3 spaces):** the Forsaken controls the world.

**Then all Fates are revealed**, and each player gains 1 bonus Glory for every Host card in their hand and Choir that matches the winning faction's alignment.

**The winner** is the player with the most Glory *among those whose Fate matches the controlling faction.* Everyone else, however rich, has backed the wrong world.

**The Unclaimed Divide.** If the marker finishes in the Divide but the Forsaken token was never drawn at setup, no faction controls the world. In that case the restriction lifts: the highest Glory total at the table wins outright, regardless of Fate. No endgame card bonus is awarded in this scenario.

## **The Nine Celestials**

Each player commands one Celestial, a public, asymmetric power. A Celestial power never consumes your turn action. Unless the card says otherwise, it may be used once per Day, only in its listed timing window. Powers written as standing rules (such as the Dominion's larger Choir) are always in effect.

| Celestial | Power |
| ----- | ----- |
| **The Tempest** | Destroy 1 Host card from the Altar. |
| **The Oracle** | During the Unveiling, look at the top card of the Event deck. You may choose to play it or bury it at the bottom of the deck. |
| **The Eclipse** | During the Reaping, choose one Host card in your Choir and treat it as Neutral. |
| **The Cantor** | During your turn, you may Offer 2 cards to the Altar instead of 1, gaining 1 Glory for each. |
| **The Patron** | Give a Host card from your hand to another player. If their Choir is full, they must discard a card of their choice to make room for it. |
| **The Echo** | Copy the ability of a Host card currently in another player's Choir. |
| **The Dominion** | Your Choir limit is four instead of three. |
| **The Whisper** | During the Unveiling, publicly predict the movement of the Tide (0, 1, 2, or 3 during Armageddon). In the Evening, if correct, your Choir scores as if aligned with the Tide regardless of its actual alignment. |
| **The Arbiter** | You never pull or pay Glory to the pot. If you gain Glory, choose a player to take it from. If you lose Glory, choose a player to give it to. |

## **Event Cards**

One Event is revealed each Unveiling. It modifies the rules for that entire Day and applies equally to all players. Unless an Event says otherwise, all normal rules remain in effect.

| Event | Effect |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Creation** | During the Unveiling, reveal 3 Host cards as Seed to the Altar instead of 1. |
| **Pandemonium** | All Offers to the Altar are made facedown, including the Seed, and Gathers are made blind. In the Evening, reveal all Altar cards before Setting the Tide. |
| **False Prophecy** | Set the Tide and move the Balance marker as normal. During the Reaping, score as if the Tide ran the opposite direction. |
| **Famine** | The Reaping is skipped. No Glory is gained or lost from Choir Alignment. All other scoring (Offers, card and Celestial abilities) is unaffected. |
| **Manna** | Each player draws 1 card; hand limit is 4 this Day. Discard back to 3 in the Evening. |
| **Dispersion** | Each player passes their entire hand to the player on their left. |
| **Babel** | No table talk, deals, or Glory transfers this Day. |
| **Fall From Grace** | Dark Offerings count double when Setting the Tide. |
| **Ascension** | Light Offerings count double when Setting the Tide. |
| **Armageddon** | The Balance marker may move up to 3 spaces this Day. |
| **The Plague** | Each player discards 1 Host card from their Choir and replaces it from the top of the deck. |
| **Tithe** | Pay 1 Glory for each Host ability you activate this Day. |
| **The Flood** | Light Host cards in Choirs cannot activate this Day. Neutral cards still activate. |
| **Let There Be Light** | Dark Host cards in Choirs cannot activate this Day. Neutral cards still activate. |
| **The Sabbath** | The Balance marker does not move this Day. The Reaping still occurs. |
| **The Fast** | No Glory may be spent this Day. Exalt and Turn are unavailable. |
| **Equilibrium** | During the Unveiling, move the Balance marker 1 space toward the center. No effect if it is already on the center space. |
| **Divine Calling** | Exalt costs 0 Glory this Day. If your Choir is full, you still pay 1 Glory to discard a Host card and Exalt in its place. |
| **Purgatory** | This Day, ignore the Tide during the Reaping. Each net-Neutral Choir gains 1 Glory; each Aligned Choir loses 1 Glory. |
| **Casting Lots** | During the Unveiling, each player must secretly predict the direction of the Tide (Light, Dark, or Neutral). In the Evening, reveal: a correct prediction gains 1 Glory (2 if you called Neutral); an incorrect prediction loses 1 Glory. |

## **Advice for Your First Game**

Offer early and often; Glory is slow to earn and everything costs it. Watch what people Gather more than what they Offer, because taking a card off the public Altar into a private Choir is the most honest move anyone makes all Day. Don't panic about hiding your Fate in the first two Days; the Reaping rewards riding the Tide no matter your allegiance, so playing "out of character" is often just good play. And remember the Altar is a vote that can be stolen: the last players to act each Day have enormous power over where the world moves.

## **Glossary**

**Abyss.** The four spaces at the dark end of the Balance. If the tracking marker finishes here, the Fallen control the world.

**Activate.** After completing your action, trigger the ability of a Host card in your Choir. Each Host may be activated once per turn.

**Alignment.** The net difference between the Light and Darkness values of the Host cards in your Choir. A net toward Light is a Light Alignment, a net toward Dark is a Dark Alignment, and an equal split is Neutral (net zero). Scored during the Reaping.

**Altar.** The shared central pool of Host cards. Cards arrive via the Seed and Offers, and leave via Gathers. The Altar's contents set the Tide each Evening and are then discarded.

**Balance, the.** The 11-space tug-of-war track. The center 3 spaces are the Divide. The 4 spaces at each end are the Heights (Light) and the Abyss (Dark). The tracking marker's final position decides which faction controls the world.

**Celestial.** The unique, asymmetrical character each player commands. A Celestial power is free, does not use your turn action, and triggers only in its listed timing window.

**Choir.** Your personal tableau of Host cards (limit 3, or 4 for the Dominion). Its Alignment is scored each Reaping, and its cards may be Activated once per turn.

**Darkness.** One of the two opposed Offering types, paired against Light. Dark Offerings push the Tide toward the Abyss, and Dark Host cards give your Choir a Dark Alignment.

**Day.** A full round of play. A game lasts up to 6 Days, each split into Morning and Evening.

**Declarations.** The second step of the Unveiling. The Whisper makes their public Tide prediction, and if Casting Lots is in play every player commits a secret one. All declarations happen before the Seed is revealed.

**Divide.** The center 3 spaces of the Balance. If the marker finishes here, the Forsaken control the world. (If no Forsaken is in play, see the Unclaimed Divide.)

**Evening.** The second phase of a Day. In order: Resolve Evening Abilities, Set the Tide, resolve the Reaping, Refresh the Altar, Upkeep.

**Exalt.** An action: pay 1 Glory to play a Host card from your hand into your Choir. If your Choir is full, you may discard a card and pay an additional 1 Glory to replace it.

**Facedown.** A Host card turned face down. In a Choir it is inert, with no ability, no Offering value, and no contribution to Alignment. On the Altar it is hidden but active: its Offering still counts toward the Tide, and it is revealed each Evening before Setting the Tide.

**Faithful.** The Light faction. Wins by driving the Balance into the Heights.

**Fallen.** The Dark faction. Wins by driving the Balance into the Abyss.

**Fate.** Your secret role, either Faithful, Fallen, or Forsaken. Drawn at setup and revealed only at game end, or when you Turn.

**Forsaken.** The neutral faction. Wins by keeping the Balance in the Divide. Cannot Turn.

**Gather.** An action: take 1 Host card from the Altar or the top of the Host deck into your Choir. If your Choir is full, you may discard a card and pay 1 Glory to replace it.

**Glory.** The game's points and its currency. Earned through actions and scoring, spent on Exalt and the Turn, transferable to other players in deals, and drawn from or paid into the Pot. Cannot drop below 0.

**Heights.** The four spaces at the light end of the Balance. If the marker finishes here, the Faithful control the world.

**Host card.** The primary card type. Each has a unique ability and an Offering value (+1 or +2 Light, Neutral, or +1 or +2 Darkness). The 66-card deck is split evenly: 22 Light, 22 Dark, and 22 Neutral or Variable. Host cards fill hands, the Altar, and Choirs.

**Light.** One of the two opposed Offering types, paired against Darkness. Light Offerings push the Tide toward the Heights, and Light Host cards give your Choir a Light Alignment.

**Morning.** The first phase of a Day: the Unveiling, then each player takes one turn clockwise.

**Neutral.** An Offering or Alignment value of zero. Neutral cards never move the Tide and never contribute to Alignment, but they still fill slots and may carry abilities.

**Offer.** An action: play 1 Host card from your hand onto the Altar and gain 1 Glory. An Offer is provisional, and any player may Gather it away before the round ends.

**Offering value.** The Light, Dark, or Neutral strength (+1 or +2) printed on a Host card. It affects the Tide while on the Altar and Alignment while in a Choir.

**Pot.** The shared Glory supply that players draw Glory from and pay it into.

**Reaping, the.** The Evening scoring step. Compare your Choir's Alignment to the Tide. A match gains Glory equal to your net Alignment, opposition loses that much, and a Neutral Choir neither gains nor loses. Gains and losses from Alignment are capped at 3 Glory per Reaping.

**Refresh the Altar.** The Evening step that discards all remaining Altar cards.

**Replacement.** Swapping a card into a full Choir is part of the Gather and Exalt actions, never a separate action. On a Gather you pay 1 Glory to replace, and on an Exalt you pay an additional 1 Glory to replace.

**Seed.** The Host card revealed onto the Altar during the Unveiling to start the round's pool. (Creation reveals 3 Seeds. Pandemonium places the Seed facedown.)

**Set the Tide.** The Evening step that totals the Altar's Light against its Darkness to determine the Tide and move the Balance marker, by margin, up to 2 spaces.

**Target.** An ability Targets a card when it requires you to choose a specific Host card in another player's Choir, such as to turn it Facedown, replace it, discard it, or copy it. A card that cannot be Targeted is immune to those effects.

**Tide.** The round's prevailing direction, either Light, Dark, or Neutral. It is set each Evening by the Altar's totals, then moves the Balance and governs Reaping scoring.

**Turn (action).** Once per game, spend your action and 1 Glory to defect to the opposing faction by placing a Turn card in your Choir. The Forsaken cannot Turn. Your new Fate takes effect at the next Unveiling, and a Turn on the final Day does not resolve in time.

**Turn card.** The blank, permanent card placed when you Turn. It is not a Host card. It has no Offering value, no Alignment, and no ability, and it cannot be Targeted, replaced, or counted by any effect that references Host cards.

**Unclaimed Divide.** If the game ends in the Divide but no Forsaken token was drawn at setup, no faction controls the world. The highest total Glory wins outright, regardless of Fate.

**Unveiling, the.** The fixed opening sequence of each Morning, resolved before any turns: (1) the Event, (2) Declarations, (3) the Seed.

**Upkeep.** The final Evening step. Each player draws back up to 3 cards, then the starting player marker passes left.

**Used Card (Sideways).** A once-per-game card whose ability has been spent is turned sideways. It keeps its Offering value and Alignment but loses its ability, and stays in its slot until replaced or moved.
