# **THE DIVIDE FOR THREE**

*A variant of open ambition. No secrets, no factions, no mercy.*

**Players:** 3 | **Play Time:** 30 to 45 minutes

---

## **What Changes**

With three Celestials at the table, there are no hidden loyalties and no world to claim. The Balance, the Fates, and the Turn are set aside. What remains is the contest itself: six Days, six Tides, and three rivals fighting in the open over which way each Day falls. Every Choir is public, every Glory total is known, and the only hidden things left are the cards in your hand.

**To win, you need one thing: the most Glory when the sixth Evening ends.**

---

## **Setup Changes**

1. **Remove from the game:** the Balance track and marker, all Fate tokens, all Turn cards, and these five Host cards: **Revelation, Knowledge, Bounty, Flaming Sword, and Belial**. Remove these three Event cards: **The Sabbath, Equilibrium, and Armageddon**. This leaves a Host deck of 64 and an Event deck of 17.
2. **All other setup is unchanged.** Each player receives a Celestial, a hidden hand of 3 Host cards, and a face-up Choir of 3. Glory starts at 0.

If any surviving card mentions a Fate or the Turn, ignore that text. The rest of the card works as printed.

---

## **What Stays the Same**

The Day runs exactly as written: the Unveiling, one turn per player, then the Evening. Your actions are **Offer, Gather, and Exalt**. The Tide is set from the Altar as normal, and the Reaping scores every Choir against the Tide's direction, with the usual cap of 3. Deals and table talk remain open, and Glory still moves freely between players.

Only one thing the Tide used to do is gone: the marker no longer moves, because there is no marker. Each Day's Tide matters once, at that Day's Reaping, and then the Altar is swept clean.

---

## **Ending the Game**

The game always lasts the full 6 Days. There is no early end.

After the sixth Reaping, each player gains 1 bonus Glory for every Host card in their hand and Choir whose Offering matches the direction of the **final Day's Tide**. If the final Tide is Neutral, no bonus is awarded.

**The most Glory wins.** If players tie, the tied player with more cards matching the final Tide wins. If still tied, they share the dawn.

---

## **How It Plays**

Without Fates, the war moves into the open. Your Choir tells your rivals exactly which Tide you need, so expect them to fight you for it: a well-timed Offer against your lean, a Gather that strips your card from the Altar, a Dark hand held back for the moment your Choir commits to the Light. The final Day is a battle twice over, because the last Tide sets both the last Reaping and the endgame bonus.

The Altar is small at three players. Single cards swing the Tide hard, so hold your strongest Offerings for the moment they decide the Day. If your table finds the Tides too thin, try revealing **2 Seed cards** at each Unveiling instead of 1.
