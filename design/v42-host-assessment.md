# V42 Host deck: assessment and engine synergies

November 18, 2026, after V42 went live. Measured on 3,000 bot games of the live rules (bots that value card abilities), plus the 20,000-game balance run (win ratio over cards held at the end of any Day).

## The mix today (69 cards)

|---|---|---|
| **Engine income** (pays Glory at the end of the Day) | Uriel, Beelzebub, Zealot, Twilight, Moderation, Stillness, Firmament, Veil, Apple, Eternity, Outcast, Leviathan, Hubris, Raguel, Confession, Moloch, Providence, Mammon, Favor, Seraphim, Legion | 21 |
| **Bets** (+1 or −1) | Vessel, Prophecy, Bounty | 3 |

The spread across roles is good. The problems are inside the engine group and in a handful of cards that rarely matter.

## What works

- **Every card is in range** (0.77–1.26) and the game hits its targets: about 7 Days, the winner at 1.4–1.5 Glory a Day, all three actions used.
- **Strong archetypes already exist:** sets (Uriel, Beelzebub, Zealot), stores (Providence, Mammon, Favor), and the coalition tools (Raguel, Bounty, Scapegoat, Leviathan, Temptation).

## What doesn't

### 1. The engine is flat

Of the 21 engine cards, 17 pay a flat +1 on a condition that has nothing to do with your other cards. Building means stacking independent +1s, not assembling a machine. Only a few cards pay off together:

| Synergy today | Cards |
|---|---|
| Same alignment | Uriel or Beelzebub, Zealot, Seraphim or Legion |
| Neutral Choir | Moderation, Fig Leaves, Nephilim |
| A mixed Choir | Twilight, Nephilim |

**Missing:**
- Cards that feed the engine (more cards to Exalt).
- Cards that reward a style of play rather than a single condition.
- Anything that turns other players' actions into your fuel.

### 2. Dead or nearly dead cards

| Card | Problem |
|---|---|
| **Snare** | Never fired. Offers are facedown, so nobody Gathers your Offer knowingly. |
| **Council** | Its own swap is now just a free Exalt (every Exalt is free), so only the 1-Glory toll from other players is left. Rarely used. |
| **Lilith** | Protects your hand, but only Gabriel and Serpent touch hands now. |
| **Hymn, Lamentation** | "Offer 2 cards face up for one reward" is almost never worth it. The ability barely fires. |
| **Forger** | Gather from the discard pile. Niche, 0.80. |
| **Wisdom** | Peek at the top card, and you may discard it. Rarely changes anything. |

### 3. Overlaps

| Overlap | Note |
|---|---|
| Wisdom, Watchers, Jubilee | Three ways to improve your hand; none touches the core tension |
| Cherubim and Tribute | Both protect your Choir. Fine now that Temptation, Parasite and Michael attack Choirs |
| Covenant and Eternity | Both reward not Offering (a pact and a solo version). Acceptable |
| Gabriel and Serpent | Gabriel is Serpent without the swap |

## +2 cards: worth bringing back, on two cards

Under V42 the *size* of your lean no longer changes your score. A +2 value still matters in three places:

- **On the Altar:** a +2 Offer is the biggest single push on the Tide, and with ±3 for a lone Choir, one push can decide a Day.
- **In your Choir:** a +2 card anchors your lean. Temptation, Parasite or one Morning Star flip cannot easily pull you off your side.
- **Combos:**
  - Morning Star on your own +2 card swings your lean by 4, a full switch.
  - Temptation can give a rival a +2 card to drag them across.
  - Raphael and Azazel can destroy a face-up +2.

A +2 is worth it only if it costs something. The cleanest cost is publicity: a loud card everyone sees.

**Proposal: Hymn and Lamentation become the two +2 cards.** *"Light +2 (Dark +2). When you Offer this card, it goes face up."* The biggest vote in the game, cast in public. Their current ability (Offer 2 face up for one reward) is almost never used.

## Proposed reworks, using the dead cards (no new cards, all existing art)

| Card | Today | Proposed | The engine or synergy it creates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hymn** (Light) | You may Offer 2 Light cards face up for one reward | **Light +2.** When you Offer this card, it goes face up. | The anchor and the loud vote (see above) |
| **Lamentation** (Dark) | The same, for Dark | **Dark +2.** When you Offer this card, it goes face up. | Hymn's mirror |
| **Wisdom** (Light) | Peek at the top card; you may discard it | **When you Exalt, draw 1 card.** | **Fuel for the Exalt engine.** With Seraphim or Legion every Exalt pays 1 and refills; with Moloch you can Exalt, sacrifice and Exalt again; it finds the cards your sets need |
| **Snare** (Dark) | Gain 1 when someone Gathers your Offer (never happens) | **When another player looks at, flips, or discards a facedown Altar card you placed, gain 1 Glory.** | **The hidden-Altar engine.** It punishes Knowledge, Belial, Revelation and Winnowing, and pairs with Vessel (more facedown cards), Veil (pays for a hidden Altar) and Mirage (protects them) |
| **Council** (Neutral) | Swap a card; others may pay you 1 to swap | **In the Morning: You may name another player. If both of your Choirs lean with the Tide at the end of the Day, each of you gains 1 Glory.** | **The alliance engine.** The only card that rewards *forming* a partnership: a pair now earns what a lone Choir does, 2 each against 3. It pairs with Raguel and Covenant, and gives Scapegoat and Temptation a target |
| **Forger** (Neutral) | Gather from the discard pile | **When you Exalt, you may Exalt a card from the discard pile instead of your hand.** | **The recycler.** It brings back spent cards (Michael, Free Will, Abaddon, Deliverance, Mercy), the set pieces you lost, and cards Michael or Temptation knocked out |
| **Lilith** (Dark) | Other abilities cannot touch your hand | **When a card is discarded from another player's Choir, you may put it into your hand.** | **The scavenger.** Rivals' Moloch sacrifices, Michael attacks and full-Choir discards become your next Exalt. Pairs with Forger and Wisdom |

Six of the seven cut redundant or idle abilities. The seventh, Lilith, is weak; its hand protection is the only loss, and Gabriel and Serpent stay counterable by playing a hand you don't mind being seen.

## The engines after the reworks

| Engine | How it works | Key cards |
|---|---|---|
| **The Faithful set** | A full Choir of one alignment pays every Day | Uriel or Beelzebub (+2), Zealot (+1), Seraphim or Legion (+1 per Exalt), Wisdom (refuel), Hymn or Lamentation (anchor) |
| **The hoard** | Glory piles up on cards; cash in at the right moment | Providence, Mammon, Favor, Defiance; threatened by Michael, Temptation and Parasite |
| **The hidden Altar** | Win from the shadows, punish anyone who peeks | Vessel, Veil, Mirage, Snare, Knowledge |
| **The broker** | Tie yourself to others and profit from their fate | Council, Raguel, Covenant, Bounty, Leviathan, Hubris |
| **The scavenger** | Live off what others lose | Lilith, Forger, Moloch, Michael, Temptation, Absolution |
| **The lone wolf** | Bet on being alone: +3 or −3 | Heresy, Morning Star (with a +2 card), Fig Leaves, Mercy, Ordeal, Acolyte |
| **The Neutral** | Stay out of the war and collect | Moderation, Stillness, Nephilim, Fig Leaves, Twilight, Outcast |

## Watch

- **Ordeal** doubles a lone result to ±6. That's a deliberate gamble, but it breaks "4 is a flawless Day". It could cap at 4.
- **Apple** (0.78) and **Fig Leaves** (0.77) are at the bottom. Bots rarely switch sides on purpose, so both should do better with people.

## Next

Test the seven reworks together (bots taught to use them), measure the balance and the game's shape, then go card by card.
