# Card assessment for the coalition game (V41 draft)

For Robert's review, November 17, 2026. Every Open Game Host card checked against the rules below, given one clear job, and kept or reworked. Offering values (Light +1, Dark +2 and so on) are unchanged, so the deck's Light and Dark totals stay equal and no card needs new art.

## The rules these cards are written for

- **Scoring (coalitions):** at the Reaping, the Choirs leaning with the Tide score: **alone 3; two, 2 for the stronger lean and 1 for the weaker (a tie: 1 each); three or more, 0.** Leaning against the Tide: **lose 1**. A Neutral Choir neither scores nor loses.
- **Your lean is your team.** Its size only decides who gets the 2 in a pair.
- **Choir:** start with **2 random cards**, room for **4**.
- **Offer:** hand to Altar, facedown, gain 1 Glory (unchanged).
- **Gather:** Altar or deck to Choir, free (unchanged; a replaced card is discarded).
- **Exalt:** hand to Choir, **costs 1 Glory**; a replaced card is **discarded**, so cards keep rotating.
- One turn per Day, no Seed. Base game: no Celestials, no Events.

## The jobs

| Job | What it gives a player | Cards |
|---|---|---|
| **Engine: alignment synergy** | pays more the more of your color you hold; finished by Exalting | Uriel, Beelzebub, Seraphim, Legion, Zealot |
| **Engine: store** | Glory piles up on the card; cash it in when you choose | Providence, Mammon |
| **Engine: growth** | weak early, strong late | Tree of Life, Leviathan, Eternity |
| **Engine: steady income** | +1 under a condition, every Day | Twilight, Moderation, Stillness, Firmament, Outcast |
| **Engine support** | draws, recycles and swaps that make the engine run | Temptation, Council, Moloch, Absolution, Forger, Jubilee |
| **Information** | see what others hide | Wisdom, Gabriel, Serpent, Ithuriel, Urim, Watchers, Prophecy, Confession |
| **Altar** | push, protect, destroy or retake Altar cards | Hymn, Lamentation, Raphael, Azazel, Sanctuary, Remnant, Vessel, Abaddon, Snare, Asmadai, Mirage |
| **Team tools** | change, hide or exploit which side you are on at the Reaping | Heresy, Morning Star, Fig Leaves, Mercy, Scapegoat, Ordeal, Martyr, Apple, Raguel, Shroud, Nephilim |
| **Interaction** | hurt, bind or slow other players | Michael, Covenant, Anathema, Favor, Envy, Parasite, Winnowing, Silence, Deliverance |
| **Protection** | stop other players' cards | Tribute, Cherubim, Veil, Lilith |
| **Tempo** | an extra action, once | Free Will, Defiance |

Light and Dark cards mirror each other job for job (Uriel and Beelzebub, Providence and Mammon, Tree of Life and Leviathan, Seraphim and Legion, Free Will and Defiance, Mercy and Scapegoat, Hymn and Lamentation, Raphael and Azazel, Covenant and Anathema, Tribute and Lilith).

## Totals

- **19 reworks**, the rest wording only.
- **Engine cards: 21**: 5 synergy, 2 stores, 3 growth, 5 steady income, 6 support.
- **Information: 8. Altar: 11. Team tools: 11. Interaction: 9. Protection: 4. Tempo: 2.**

## How a game should feel

- **Days 1 to 3:** Choirs have 2 or 3 cards. Offers and the Tide are the main income; players pick sides, and the first pairs form.
- **Days 4 to 6:** growth cards switch on, stores have 3 or 4 Glory on them, and synergy Choirs reach 3 cards of a color. Exalt becomes the key action, worth 1 Glory to land the exact card that completes a set.
- **Day 7 on:** a finished engine pays 2 to 4 Glory a Day. Offers now matter mostly for deciding who wins the Tide, and team tools (Heresy, Fig Leaves, Mercy, Scapegoat) swing the last Reapings.

## Things to watch

- **One-color Choirs.** Synergy rewards them, which goes against the old principle "no player should profit from playing only one alignment." Coalition scoring is the check: a one-color Choir announces its team loudly, and crowding punishes it. Watch whether it is enough.
- **Stores** can be attacked (Michael, Parasite, Deliverance); that is intended.
- **Neutral engines** (Twilight, Moderation, Stillness) let a player sit out the Tide and still earn. Watch that sitting out never beats taking a side.
- **Raguel's alliance** pays you when the player you Bound gains, which may tempt two players into permanent partnership. That is good for the game if it stays breakable.
