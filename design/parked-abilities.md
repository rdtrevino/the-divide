# Parked abilities

Versions Robert liked but did not pick during the Host card rework (November 2026). These are candidates to bring back during the final walkthrough of the deck if a card needs replacing or a slot opens up. Each one has a test switch in the engine so it can be measured again.

| Card | Parked ability | Win ratio | Test switch | Why it was parked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Winnowing (Dark) | On your turn: Discard a random card from another player's hand. They don't draw back up until the end of the Day. | 0.76 | `winnowing-nodraw` | Robert preferred the blind Altar discard, but likes this one. |
| Zealot | At the end of the Day: If more Altar cards were face up than facedown when it was revealed, gain 1 Glory. (A mirror of Veil.) | 1.16 | `zealot-open` | Robert picked "3 or more Choir cards, all of one alignment", but likes this one. |
