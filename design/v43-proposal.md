# V43 proposal: a Host deck built around engines

November 18, 2026. For Robert's review; nothing here is built or tested yet. Starts from the live V42 deck (69 cards, every card 0.77–1.26) and the assessment in `v42-host-assessment.md`.

## What makes engine games work, and what this deck needs

| Principle | Where it comes from | What it means here |
|---|---|---|
| **Several lanes, each findable from a random start** | Race for the Galaxy, Wingspan | Each engine needs a clear payoff card and enablers common enough to find over about 7 Days |
| **Enablers feed several payoffs, and payoffs use several enablers** | Dominion, Terraforming Mars | The fun is the unexpected combination, so most engine cards should help two lanes |
| **Engines can be attacked and read** | Root, Cosmic Encounter | Choirs are public, so every engine needs a counter in the deck (Michael, Temptation, Parasite, Silence) |
| **Some pay early, some pay late** | Dominion's money versus engine | Today almost everything pays +1 from the first Day. This adds both slow-growing and fast-fading cards |
| **Every action has a payoff card** | Action-selection games | Offer, Gather, Exalt and the facedown Altar each need a card that rewards them |
| **No memory load, short text** | Robert's rule | Everything is checked on the table: Glory on a card, a card turned toward you, public leans |

| **Streak** | **Favor** (3 winning Days in a row) | Fig Leaves (sitting out keeps the streak), Heresy, Mercy | Anything that pushes you off the Tide |

**Bold** marks a reworked card. Every approved engine has a home: Light and Dark sets, hoard, growth, churn, ascetic, diversity, broker, hidden Altar, seer, toll, infiltrator, relic, sacrifice, recycling, catch-up, Neutral, switching, streak.

### Combinations to discover

- **The martyr loop:** Exalt into a full Choir (the replaced card is discarded, Martyr +1), then Moloch discards another (+1, Martyr +1). Lilith players at the table can take what you throw away.
- **The +2 swing:** Morning Star on your own Hymn moves your lean by 4, a full switch for free. Temptation hands a rival your Lamentation to drag them across.
- **The bait:** Eternity keeps a face-up Hymn on the Altar, a +2 standing vote. Anyone who Gathers it to stop it pays your Snare. Raphael or Azazel can destroy it, at the cost of their Offer's reward.
- **The ascetic gambit:** Remnant with a Choir of 2 pays 2 a Day, so rivals answer by filling your Choir with Temptation and Parasite. You answer by sacrificing with Moloch, and Martyr pays you for it.
- **The protected hoard:** Defiance means a Michael aimed at your Providence pays you its Glory anyway. With Cherubim it can't be touched at all.
- **The broker table:** Council names a partner in the Morning; Tribute pays you whenever an ally on your side Exalts; Raguel ties your fate to theirs. Then Scapegoat or Bounty from a rival can break the pact.
- **The diverse Choir:** Twilight, Nephilim and any Neutral card pay 2 a Day with only three cards.
- **The seer's read:** Confession forces a hand open; Gabriel predicts from it; Serpent can swap a card in so the prediction comes true.

## Round 5 (November 18): Landslide finds a home

Robert likes Landslide but not on Favor. Firmament's old ability (the Tide won by exactly 1) and Landslide are mirrors about the margin, so Firmament takes Landslide.

| Version | Favor | Firmament | Spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landslide on Favor; Firmament unchanged | 1.28 | 1.01 | 0.107 |
| Straight swap: Firmament Landslide, Favor the narrow win | 1.37 (a Dark +1 card gives its holder a side, so it pays far more often than on a Neutral card) | 0.91 | 0.112 |
| **Firmament Landslide; Favor the upset** | **1.17** | **0.92** | **0.101**, every card within 0.8–1.2 |

- **Firmament:** At the end of the Day: If the Tide won by 3 or more and your Choir leans with it, gain 1 Glory.
- **Favor:** At the end of the Day: If your Choir leans with the Tide and more Choirs leaned against it than with it, gain 1 Glory.
