## 2. Issues found

**Terminology.** V32 used "Current" on 7 cards and "Tide" on 8; the rulebook and July 25 prints said Tide.

**Timing collision.** Evening abilities resolved before the Tide was set, but Stillness, Firmament, Prophecy, Heresy, and Fig Leaves needed the Tide.

**Targeting vocabulary.** Cherubim and Veil used six specific verbs; Tribute, Lilith, and the glossary used "Target".

**V32 vs July 25 prints.** Colon cadence never printed; Shroud "Alignment" vs printed "Offering"; Council "If a unanimous yes"; Parasite dropped Turn; the Dominion gained a starting-hand line.

**Possibly unintended V32 changes.** Raphael lost Once per Day (Azazel kept it); Michael could target any Choir; Gabriel dropped "Do not reveal it"; Nephilim "and" to "or"; Raguel lost its entry trigger; Prophecy paid on the Tide.

**Typos.** Veil "of Flip"; Uriel "At Reaping:"; lowercase after colons; Raguel "choir"; face-down spelled three ways; "Tree Of Life".

**Rulebook vs cards.** Eclipse timing; Whisper predicted movement in the rulebook but direction on the card; glossary said Fate is revealed on Turn but the Turn rules did not; no Pot in Components; printed Fate counts 4/4/2 vs a maximum need of 3/3/1.

**Rules gaps.** Winner tiebreak; Forsaken endgame bonus; Nephilim and Shroud at game end; Arbiter and Mammon against 0 Glory; Hubris with tied leaders; Tithe scope; Silence timing.

**3-player variant gaps.** Events that move the Balance marker; deck balance after cutting the Fate and Turn cards; Veil mentioning Fate.

## 3. Resolution

All Open Game issues were decided on November 13 and applied in V33 and rulebook v4. Four Hidden Roles questions remain open and are listed in `design-decisions.md`.
