# **THE DIVIDE: Rulebook**

*A game of divine ambition, open rivalry, and hidden loyalties.*

**Created by:** Robert Treviño
**Version:** Rulebook v14, for card data V44
**Players:** 3 to 6 (the Open Game at 3 or 4, Hidden Roles at 5 or 6) | **Play Time:** 45 to 75 minutes
**Mechanisms:** Engine Building, Negotiation, Shifting Alliances, Hidden Information; Hidden Allegiance and Tug-of-War in Hidden Roles

---

## **Overview**

Before the first dawn, the unfinished world rests on the Divide, the narrow span between the Heights above and the Abyss below. You are a power of Heaven or the Abyss, competing for **Glory**.

* **Everyone feeds one shared Altar.** Each Day, players put cards on the Altar, many of them facedown.
* **The Altar sets the Tide.** Each Evening, the Altar's Light is weighed against its Darkness. The heavier side is the Tide: **Light**, **Dark**, or **Neutral**.
* **Your Choir is your team.** Your Choir is a row of up to four face-up cards. The way it leans, Light or Dark, is the side you are on, and everyone can see it.
* **The Tide pays the few, not the many.** Alone, you win or lose big: a Choir that leans with the Tide by itself gains 3 Glory, and a Choir that leans against it by itself loses 3. Otherwise it is 1: two Choirs with the Tide gain 1 each, and every Choir against it loses 1. If three or more lean with it, none of them gains. So you need allies to win the Tide, and you need them to be few.
* **Build now or sway the Altar.** Each turn you either put a card on the Altar for Glory now, or build your Choir into a machine that pays more as the game goes on. Building costs nothing but the card.
* **First to 12 ends it.** The game ends after the first Reaping at which anyone has 12 Glory or more. The most Glory wins.

## **What's in the Box**

* **69 Host cards:** 23 Light, 23 Dark, and 23 Neutral or both sides.
* **9 Celestial cards** *(Advanced)*: unique player powers.
* **20 Event cards** *(Advanced)*: one revealed each Day, changing that Day's rules. The Advanced game uses 17 of them.
* **Glory markers** and **1 starting player marker.**
* **For Hidden Roles only:** the Balance (an 11-space track with 1 marker), Fate tokens (Faithful, Fallen, Forsaken), and Turn cards.

### **The Places on the Table**

| Place | What it is |
| ----- | ----- |
| **The Altar** | A shared pool of cards in the center. First Fruits sit face up; every Offer goes on facedown, so no one is sure what the Altar holds until the Evening reveals it. Each Evening its totals set the Tide. It is emptied every Evening, and any card on it, hidden or not, can be taken by another player before the Day ends. |
| **Your Choir** | Your row of up to 4 face-up cards; you start with 2. Its lean is your side, its cards give you abilities, and each Evening it is scored against the Tide. |
| **Your hand** | Your hidden Host cards, normally 3, refilled each Evening. Your First Fruits come from it each Morning, and your facedown Offers keep its secrets a little longer. |
| **Glory** | Both score and currency. You earn it from Offers, the Reaping and your Choir's abilities, and a few abilities cost it. Glory comes from and returns to a shared supply called the **Pot**, and never drops below 0. Glory and cards pass between players only when a card says so. Some cards (Providence, Mammon) store Glory on themselves; it is not yours until you take it. |

### **Reading a Host Card**

The Host deck is the game's one deck of playable cards. Every Host card shows:

* **A name and an ability.** An ability begins with when it works: *In the Morning*, *On your turn*, *In the Evening* (before the Altar is revealed), or *At the end of the Day*. An ability with no label is always on, or starts with "When…" and works whenever that happens. Some cards say "then discard this card": they are **spent** when used.
* **An Offering value:** Light +1, Dark +1, or Neutral; Hymn and Lamentation are +2. On the Altar, a card's Offering pushes the Tide. In your Choir, it shapes your lean. Neutral cards push nothing and lean nowhere. Nephilim counts as both, and Acolyte takes the side of yesterday's Tide.

## **Setup**

1. **Shuffle the Host deck** (69 cards).
2. **Deal Host cards.** Each player takes a hidden hand of 3 Host cards. Then deal each player 2 more Host cards face up in a row in front of them: their starting Choir.
3. **Finish.** Set all Glory to 0 and give a random player the starting player marker.

**Advanced game:** also shuffle the Event deck, without The Sabbath, Equilibrium, and Armageddon (17 Events), and deal each player a Celestial, face up. The Dominion takes a hand of 5.

---

## **A Day at a Glance**

The game is played in rounds called **Days**. Every Day has three phases: **Morning** prepares the Altar, at **Noon** each player takes a turn, and in the **Evening** the Altar is revealed and every Choir is scored. Days continue until someone reaches 12 Glory.

| Phase | Step | What happens |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Morning** | 1. The Event *(Advanced)* | Reveal the Day's Event. Its rule applies to everyone, all Day. |
| | 2. Predictions | Abilities marked *In the Morning* (such as Prophecy, Gabriel, Council) are used now. A card kept with Eternity returns to the Altar, face up. |
| | 3. First Fruits | Everyone reveals 1 card from hand onto the Altar at once, then draws 1. |
| **Noon** | Turns | Each player, clockwise from the first player: one action, then activate your Choir. |
| **Evening** | 1. Reveal the Altar | First, abilities marked *In the Evening* (Fig Leaves; in the Advanced game, the Eclipse). Then turn every facedown Altar card face up. |
| | 2. Set the Tide | Total the Altar's Light and Darkness. The heavier side is the Tide. |
| | 3. The Reaping | Score every Choir, then resolve *At the end of the Day* abilities. |
| | 4. Refresh the Altar | Discard every card left on the Altar (except one kept with Eternity). |
| | 5. Upkeep | Draw (or discard) to your hand limit of 3; pass the starting player marker left. |

---

## **Morning**

1. **The Event** *(Advanced game)*. The first player draws the top Event card, reads it aloud, and places it face up. It changes the rules for the whole Day and applies to everyone equally.
2. **Predictions.** Abilities marked *In the Morning* are used now, before any card reaches the Altar.
3. **First Fruits.** The third step of Morning: every player reveals 1 card from hand at the same time, face up on the Altar, then draws 1. It earns no Glory and is not an Offer.

> *Example: Ada holds Michael, Moloch, and Wisdom. Her Choir leans Light, so she would like to give Michael, but Michael is a card she may want to Exalt later. She gives Wisdom (Light +1) instead, sees that Ben and Cy both gave Dark cards, and now knows the Altar starts the Day tilted toward the Dark.*

---

## **Noon: Your Turn**

At Noon, starting with the first player and moving clockwise, each player takes exactly one turn: **choose one action, then activate your Choir.**

| Action | What you do | Cost | What you get |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Offer** | Play 1 card from your hand **facedown** onto the Altar. | A card | 1 Glory now, and a hidden push on the Tide. |
| **Gather** | Take 1 card from the Altar into your Choir. | Free | A new Choir card, and one card fewer on the Altar. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards first. |
| **Exalt** | Play 1 card from your hand into your Choir. | A card | Exactly the card you want, without it ever touching the Altar. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards first. |

**Offer: Glory now, and your secret vote.** The card's Offering pulls the Tide even while hidden. The 1 Glory is the Offer's **reward**, and it is yours to keep even if the card is later taken. You may look at your own facedown cards at any time. An Offer is provisional: anyone acting after you can Gather it away before the Day ends.

**Gather: build from what is there.** You may Gather a facedown card without looking at it first; it enters your Choir face up. Taking a card off the Altar also weakens its side of the Tide. If the Altar is empty, you cannot Gather.

**Exalt: build exactly what you want.** It brings a chosen card from your hidden hand into your Choir. It costs no Glory, but you give up the Offer's Glory and its push on the Tide.

**Then activate.** After your action, you may activate each Host card in your Choir whose ability works *On your turn*. Each card activates at most once per turn.

---

## **Evening**

When every player has taken their turn, Noon is over. Resolve the Evening in this order.

1. **Reveal the Altar.** Turn every facedown card on the Altar face up.
2. **Set the Tide.** Total all Light values on the Altar, total all Darkness values, and compare. The heavier side is the **Tide**, and its **winning margin** is the difference. A tie, or an empty Altar, means a **Neutral** Tide.

   > *Example: the Altar holds Light +2, Light +1, Dark +1, Dark +1, and a Neutral card. Light totals 3, Darkness totals 2. The Tide is Light, with a winning margin of 1.*

3. **The Reaping.** Score every Choir (see below). Then resolve every ability marked *At the end of the Day*.
4. **Refresh the Altar.** Discard every card remaining on the Altar.
5. **Upkeep.** Everyone draws back up to their hand limit, normally 3 (or discards down to it), and the starting player marker passes left. If the Host deck is ever empty, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck.

Then a new Day begins.

### **Scoring Your Choir**

1. **Find your lean:** add up the Light values in your Choir, add up the Darkness values, and take the difference. The larger side is your lean (Light or Dark), and the difference is its strength. Neutral cards and facedown cards count nothing. A Choir with no difference is Neutral.
2. **Count the Choirs on each side of the Tide,** and score:

| Your Choir leans | Alone on that side | With others on that side |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **With** the Tide | gain **3 Glory** | two Choirs: gain **1 Glory** each. Three or more: **nothing** |
| **Against** the Tide | lose **3 Glory** | lose **1 Glory** each |

* A **Neutral** Choir neither gains nor loses, and does not count on either side. On a **Neutral** Tide, no one gains or loses.
* How strongly you lean does not matter, only which way.
* Glory never drops below 0.

> *Example: the Tide is Light. Ada and Ben lean Light, Cy leans Dark, and Dee's Choir is Neutral. Ada and Ben share the Tide and gain 1 each. Cy is alone against it and loses 3. Had Dee also leaned Dark, Cy and Dee would each lose only 1; had Dee leaned Light, three Choirs would share the Tide and none of them would gain.*

---

## **Ending the Game**

**The race to 12.** At the end of every Reaping, check Glory. If any player has **12 or more**, the game ends. There is no Day limit, and every player always takes the same number of turns.

**The most Glory wins.** Players who tie share the win.

---

## **How to Play Well**

**Your Choir is a public promise.** It tells the table which side you are on. Allies on your side help you win the Tide; too many allies mean no one is paid. Expect the conversation: "If you switch to Light, you'll be alone and take 3."

**Alone is a gamble.** Standing by yourself on a side pays 3 if it wins and costs 3 if it loses. A partner halves the risk and the reward.

**The facedown Altar is where the bluffing lives.** An Offer can help your side, quietly sink a partner who is getting too strong, or set a trap. First Fruits shows you one honest card from every hand each Morning; everything after that has to be read from behavior.

**Build for later.** Offers pay now; your Choir pays every Day. A Choir card you Exalt early keeps earning, so the turn you skip an Offer to build is an investment. Stores, sets and streaks (Providence, Mammon, Uriel, Beelzebub, Tree of Life) pay most to the patient.

**Advice for your first game:**

* Your starting Choir is dealt at random, so every game starts from a different lean. Build on it or switch sides; either can win.
* Watch what players discard from their Choirs: a card thrown out tells you which way they are turning.
* When three players lean the same way, someone will have to leave. Make sure it is not you, or that you leave for the better side.
* The last players to act each Day see the most of the Altar, though with facedown Offers they see less than they think.

## **Advanced: The Nine Celestials**

In the Advanced game, each player commands one Celestial, a public power. A Celestial power never uses your turn action. Its timing works like a Host card's (*In the Morning*, *On your turn*, *In the Evening*, or "When…"), each power is used at most once per Day, and powers written as standing rules (such as the Dominion's hand limit) are always in effect.

| Celestial | Power |
| ----- | ----- |
| **I. The Tempest** | When you Offer, you may destroy a face-up card on the Altar. |
| **II. The Oracle** | In the Morning: Look at the top 2 Event cards. Play one, and put the other on the bottom of the Event deck. |
| **III. The Eclipse** | In the Evening: Choose a card in your Choir. It counts as Neutral at this Day's Reaping. |
| **IV. The Cantor** | When you Offer, you may Offer a second card of the same alignment (Light, Dark, or Neutral). Both go face up, and each earns its reward. |
| **V. The Patron** | On your turn: Play a card from your hand into another player's Choir. If it is full, they discard one of its cards first. |
| **VI. The Echo** | On your turn: Use the On your turn ability of a card in another player's Choir, as if it were yours. Spent cards cannot be copied. |
| **VII. The Dominion** | Your hand limit is 5 instead of 3. Begin the game with 5 cards in hand. |
| **VIII. The Whisper** | In the Morning: Name Light or Dark aloud. At the Reaping, if the Tide matches, your Choir counts as leaning with it. |
| **IX. The Arbiter** | When you gain Glory at the Reaping, take 1 of it from another player: one whose Choir leaned against the Tide, if any of them has Glory. |

## **Rulings**

These settle questions the card text leaves open. The card text is always the starting point. They are grouped by topic; look a card up by name.

### **General**

**Activating.** To activate is to use an ability you choose to use, in the Morning, on your turn, in the Evening, or at the end of the Day. Always-on abilities are never activated. On your turn, each Choir card activates at most once.

**The most or least Glory.** When a card names the player with the most or the least Glory and several players are tied, the player using the card chooses among them, unless the card says otherwise (Envy hits every tied leader).

**Spent cards.** A card that says "then discard this card" goes to the discard pile as soon as its ability is used (Michael, Free Will, Deliverance, Abaddon, Mercy). Stores (Providence, Mammon) are discarded when you take their Glory.

**Stored Glory.** Glory on Providence or Mammon is not yours: it does not count toward 12, and it is lost if the card leaves your Choir.

**Exalt and Absolution.** The card being Exalted is set aside first, so Absolution's hand discard cannot take it.

**Tithe (Event).** Every Host ability you activate costs 1 Glory, whether in the Morning, on your turn, or at the end of the Day. Always-on abilities (such as Cherubim, Tribute, and Mirage) are never activated and cost nothing, and neither do Celestial powers, which are not Host abilities. If you cannot pay, you cannot activate.

**Purgatory (Event).** Choirs still lean with or against the Tide, for every card that asks (Tree of Life, Defiance, Providence); the ones against it just lose nothing. A Choir sitting out with Fig Leaves gains nothing.

**Divine Calling (Event).** Pays for every Exalt into your own Choir, including one from the discard pile with Forger. Playing a card into another player's Choir (Temptation, the Patron, Parasite) is not an Exalt into your Choir and pays nothing.

**The Flood and Let There Be Light.** Every ability of the named alignment is off for that Day: abilities a player would activate and abilities that work on their own (such as Martyr, Snare, or Cherubim's protection). The cards still count toward your lean. On a Let There Be Light Day, Parasite may be discarded like any other card.

**Casting Lots.** The predictions pay out at the Reaping, once the Tide is known.

**Silence.** The chosen player's Choir abilities stop working, always-on ones included, until the start of your next turn.

**Anathema and Flaming Sword.** From the end of your turn until the start of your next turn, no other player may Offer (Anathema) or Exalt or Gather (Flaming Sword).

### **The Altar and Facedown Cards**

**Facedown Offerings.** Every Offer goes on the Altar facedown. Hymn and Lamentation are the exception: they always go face up when Offered. So does the Cantor's matching pair; a single card the Cantor Offers goes facedown as usual. First Fruits and cards added by other abilities follow their own text.

**Revelation, Knowledge, Belial, and Winnowing.** Each acts on facedown Altar cards. Revelation flips one face up for everyone, then may flip a face-up card facedown. Knowledge looks privately at up to 3 facedown cards that you did not place; you may tell the table what you saw, truthfully or not. Belial looks at one facedown card and may swap it with a card from your hand: the swap changes the card but not its owner, so it stays facedown and still counts as Offered by the player who placed it. Winnowing discards up to 2 facedown cards without anyone looking at them. A card seen with Knowledge or Belial is seen once and then goes back facedown.

**Veil.** Counts the Altar at the moment it is revealed in the Evening, before anything is taken away.

**Mirage.** Protects every card you placed on the Altar (Offers, First Fruits, Vessel's card), face up or facedown, until the Evening: other players cannot Gather, flip, look at, exchange, or destroy it (Revelation, Knowledge, Belial, Winnowing, Abaddon, Raphael, Azazel, the Tempest).

**Raphael and Azazel.** To destroy, you turn your own Offering face up; you still gain its 1 Glory. Only face-up cards can be destroyed, so a facedown card is safe from them (and from the Tempest).

**Snare.** Pays for each card you placed on the Altar (an Offer, First Fruits, Vessel's card) that another player Gathers, flips (Revelation), or discards (Winnowing, Abaddon, Raphael, Azazel, the Tempest).

**Vessel.** Its card goes on the Altar facedown but is not an Offer: it earns no reward and no Offer abilities trigger (Raphael, Azazel, the Tempest, Wisdom, Gabriel's call, Hymn and Lamentation going face up). Snare still pays if another player takes or discards it. If the card has been Gathered or destroyed, or the Tide is Neutral, it neither gains nor loses.

**Remnant.** Counts the empty places in your Choir at the end of the Day: with the limit of 4, you need 2 or fewer cards, Remnant included.

**Eternity.** The kept card can be anyone's. It returns to the Altar, face up, next Morning, counts toward that Day's Tide, and can be Gathered or destroyed as usual; at the end of that Day it is cleared like any other card.

**The Tempest.** Used on a turn you Offer, after the Offer and its reward, like Raphael and Azazel: only a face-up card can be destroyed, so a facedown card is safe from it.

### **Scoring and the Reaping**

**Nephilim.** Counts as both a Light and a Dark card for any card that checks for one (Twilight, Uriel, Beelzebub, Zealot). For your lean and the Tide its two values cancel (0).

**Acolyte.** Its alignment on any Day matches the previous Day's Tide, both on the Altar and in your Choir. After a Neutral Tide, and on Day 1, it is Neutral.

**Uriel, Beelzebub, and Zealot.** They count the other face-up cards in your Choir, not themselves. Zealot needs at least 3 other cards, every one of them Light or every one of them Dark.

**Heresy and Morning Star.** Decided on your turn, before the Altar is revealed, and they last until the end of the Day. **Fig Leaves** is decided in the Evening, after every turn and before the reveal. A Choir sitting out with Fig Leaves does not count on either side of the Tide.

**Mercy.** Used at the Reaping, when yours is the only Choir against the Tide: you lose 1 instead of 3, and Mercy is discarded.

**Scapegoat.** Used at the Reaping, after the scores are known and before anyone gains: pay 1 Glory and one player who would gain from the Tide gains nothing.

**Raguel.** Looks at the Bound player's Reaping (see Gained or lost below): if they gained, you gain 1; if they lost, you lose 1.

**Bounty and Prophecy.** Named or declared in the Morning, settled at the end of the Day. Bounty looks at the target's Reaping (see Gained or lost below); a target who neither gains nor loses there costs you nothing. A Neutral Tide is a miss for Prophecy: you lose 1.

**Gained or lost.** Bounty, Raguel, Favor and Parasite look only at the Reaping: a player gained if the Reaping raised their Glory, and lost if it lowered it. Glory from Offers or other cards does not count for them.

**Hubris.** Counts all Glory the leader gained that Day, from any source. If several leaders are tied and qualify, you choose which one gives you 1.

**Tree of Life, Favor, Leviathan, and Defiance.** Tree of Life compares your Choir's side with the Tide today and yesterday. Favor counts the other players who lost Glory at that Day's Reaping. Leviathan needs the Tide's winning margin to be 3 or more. Defiance counts the Choirs leaning with and against the Tide; Neutral Choirs and Choirs sitting out count for neither.

**Apple.** Compares your Choir's lean today with your Choir's lean yesterday. A Neutral lean on either Day pays nothing.

**Gabriel.** You see the hand privately, then name Light or Dark aloud. It pays if any card that player Offers that Day has the named alignment; First Fruits and Vessel's card are not Offers.

**Council.** The named player does not have to agree. Neutral Choirs do not lean the same way as anyone.

**Twilight.** Counts the other face-up cards in your Choir; Nephilim counts as one Light and one Dark.

**Outcast.** Checked after the Choirs are scored, against everyone's Glory at that moment.

**The Whisper.** A correct prediction means your Choir counts as leaning with the Tide, whichever way it leans. A Neutral Choir still scores nothing.

**The Eclipse.** Chosen in the Evening, just before the Altar is revealed, so you choose without seeing the hidden Offerings. The chosen card is Neutral for that Day's Reaping only.

**The Arbiter.** Applies only to Glory gained from your Choir at the Reaping, 3 alone or 1 in a pair. You choose which player pays the 1 among those whose Choirs leaned against the Tide. If none of them has Glory, you choose any other player who has some; if no one does, the whole gain comes from the Pot. The Arbiter never gives Glory away.

### **Targeting and Protection**

**Targeting** is any ability aimed at another player or their cards. Its six kinds are **Look at, Reveal, Copy, Flip, Replace,** and **Discard**.

**Protection.** Cherubim stops other players from Replacing or Discarding your Choir cards, and from playing cards into your Choir. Tribute makes any player Targeting your Choir pay you 1 Glory first.

**Temptation.** Playing a card into another player's Choir is Targeting: Cherubim blocks it and Tribute must be paid. If their Choir is full, they choose which of their cards to discard.

**Parasite.** It goes straight into another player's Choir when you Exalt or Gather it (Cherubim blocks this; Tribute must be paid). Its holder cannot discard it, not even to make room; other players' abilities can still remove it. Remember who placed it: at the end of each Day, that player gains 1 if its holder lost Glory at the Reaping, and Parasite is discarded if its holder gained.

**The Patron.** Works like Temptation: playing a card into another player's Choir is Targeting, so Cherubim blocks it and Tribute must be paid. If their Choir is full, they choose which of their cards to discard.

**Martyr and Lilith.** Martyr pays for every other card discarded from your Choir, by you or anyone (Moloch, Michael, a full Choir, spent cards, store cash-ins). Lilith may take any card discarded from another player's Choir; if several players hold Lilith, the first in turn order chooses first.

### **Other Cards**

**The Echo.** Copies only *On your turn* abilities, and not spent cards (those that discard themselves).

**The Oracle.** The Event you put on the bottom is not seen by the other players. With only one Event card left in the deck, the Oracle does not look that Morning.

**The Cantor.** The second card must share the first card's alignment: Light with Light, Dark with Dark, Neutral with Neutral. Nephilim counts as both Light and Dark, so it pairs with either.

**Seraphim and Legion.** They pay when you Exalt a card of their alignment into your own Choir, not for the card itself.

**Forger and Wisdom.** Forger lets you take the Gathered or Exalted card from the discard pile instead, including spent cards. Wisdom's look happens after your Offer or Exalt; the hand limit is restored at Upkeep.

**Envy.** Usable only while the player with the most Glory has at least 3 more Glory than you, so you can never be one of the leaders it hits.

**Covenant.** Made in the Morning. If either of you Offers that Day, the pact pays nothing.

## **Hidden Roles**

*For 5 or 6 players.*

In Hidden Roles, the world itself is at stake. Every player secretly belongs to a faction, and the Tide no longer only scores your Choir: it moves a marker across the Balance, and the marker's final position decides which faction wins.

**To win, you need two things:**

1. When the game ends, the world must belong to your secret faction.
2. Among the players of that faction, you must hold the most Glory.

Faction victory makes you eligible. Glory makes you the winner. You must help your side win the world while out-earning the allies helping you do it.

### **What Changes**

All Advanced game rules apply (Celestials and Events), with these changes:

* **Use the full decks:** all 69 Host cards and all 20 Event cards.
* **Add the Balance, Fate tokens, and Turn cards.**
* **The Tide moves the Balance.** After Set the Tide, the marker moves toward the Tide: 1 space for a margin of exactly 1, or 2 spaces for a margin of 2 or more (2 is the cap). A Neutral Tide does not move it.
* **Turn** becomes a fourth action choice (see The Turn below).
* **The game lasts 6 Days, can end early,** and the winner is decided differently (see Ending a Hidden Roles Game).

### **The Balance**

An 11-space track in the center of the table with a single marker on it. The middle 3 spaces are the **Divide**. The 4 spaces on each end are the **Heights** (Light) and the **Abyss** (Dark).

### **Additional Setup**

Before dealing Celestials:

1. **Draw secret Fates.** Pool Fate tokens by player count using the table below, mix them in the bag, and have each player secretly draw one. Leave the rest in the bag, unrevealed. Because leftover tokens stay hidden, no one knows the true headcount of any faction. There may not even be a Forsaken at the table.

| Player Count | Faithful | Fallen | Forsaken | Left in Bag |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 5 Players | 3 | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| 6 Players | 3 | 3 | 1 | 1 |
| 4 Players *(optional variant)* | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 |

2. **Place the Balance** in the center with the marker on the middle space of the Divide.
3. **Set out the Turn cards** face up beside the Altar: 2 cards for 5 or 6 players (1 card in the optional 4-player variant).

### **The Turn: Defection**

Once per game, during any Day except the last, a player may spend their action to **Turn**, defecting to the opposing faction (Faithful to Fallen, or Fallen to Faithful).

* **To defect:** pay 1 Glory and place one of the face-up Turn cards from beside the Altar into your Choir. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards to clear the space.
* **The Turn card is a scar, not a tool.** It is not a Host card. It has no Offering, no Alignment, and no ability. It cannot be Targeted, discarded, replaced, or counted by any effect that references Host cards, and it occupies its Choir slot for the rest of the game.
* **Defection is not instant.** You remain your original Fate until the end of the current Day; your new Fate takes hold at the next Morning. A Turn taken on the final Day fails to resolve in time, and you score as your original Fate.
* **Defections are scarce.** The supply of Turn cards is the limit.

### **Ending a Hidden Roles Game**

The game ends **immediately** if the Balance marker reaches the final space of the Heights or the Abyss. Otherwise it ends after the Evening of the 6th Day.

1. **Who controls the world:** the marker's final position decides it. The Heights for the Faithful, the Abyss for the Fallen, the Divide for the Forsaken.
2. **Reveal all Fates.** Each player gains 1 bonus Glory for every Host card in their hand and Choir that matches the winning faction's alignment.
3. **The winner** is the player with the most Glory among those whose Fate matches the controlling faction. Everyone else, however rich, has backed the wrong world.

**The Unclaimed Divide.** If the marker finishes in the Divide but the Forsaken token was never drawn at setup, no faction controls the world. The restriction lifts: the highest Glory total at the table wins outright, regardless of Fate. No endgame card bonus is awarded in this case.

### **The Heart of Hidden Roles**

Your secret Fate wants the Balance in one place. Your Glory wants your Choir aligned with wherever the Tide is actually going. These two goals regularly pull apart.

A Fallen player might spend a Day Offering Light because their Choir leans Light and they need the points. A Faithful player might sandbag a round to avoid revealing their allegiance. The Forsaken profit most when the other factions cancel each other out. Every card you play gives the table information, and controlling how that information is read is where games are won. Don't panic about hiding your Fate in the first two Days: the Reaping rewards riding the Tide no matter your allegiance, so playing out of character is often just good play.

## **Glossary**

**Abyss.** *(Hidden Roles)* The four spaces at the dark end of the Balance. If the marker finishes here, the Fallen control the world.

**Activate.** To use a Host ability you choose to use, in the Morning, on your turn, in the Evening, or at the end of the Day. After your action, you may activate each card in your Choir whose ability works on your turn, once per turn. Always-on abilities are never activated.

**Alignment (lean).** The net difference between the Light and Darkness values of the Host cards in your Choir. A net toward Light is a Light Alignment, a net toward Dark is a Dark Alignment, and an equal split is Neutral (net zero). Scored during the Reaping.

**Altar.** The shared central pool of Host cards. Cards arrive via First Fruits and Offers, and leave via Gathers. The Altar's contents set the Tide each Evening and are then discarded.

**At the end of the Day.** A cadence. The ability resolves after the Choirs are scored at the Reaping, before the Altar is cleared.

**Balance, the.** *(Hidden Roles)* The 11-space tug-of-war track. The center 3 spaces are the Divide. The 4 spaces at each end are the Heights and the Abyss.

**Celestial.** The unique, public power each player commands. A Celestial power does not use your turn action and works only in its listed timing window.

**Choir.** Your row of up to 4 face-up Host cards; you start with 2. Its lean is your side at the Reaping, and its cards may be activated.

**Copy.** To use the ability of another player's card as if it were yours. A kind of Targeting.

**Darkness.** One of the two opposed Offering types, paired against Light. Dark Offerings push the Tide toward the Dark, and Dark Host cards give your Choir a Dark Alignment.

**Day.** A full round of play, in three phases: Morning, Noon, and Evening. The Open Game continues until a player has 12 Glory or more after a Reaping; Hidden Roles lasts 6 Days.

**Predictions.** The second step of Morning, when abilities marked *In the Morning* (such as Prophecy, the Whisper, and Casting Lots) are used, before First Fruits.

**Discard.** To move a card to the discard pile. When another player's ability discards one of your cards, it is a kind of Targeting.

**Divide.** The center 3 spaces of the Balance. *(Hidden Roles)* If the marker finishes here, the Forsaken control the world.

**Evening.** The third phase of a Day. In order: Reveal the Altar, Set the Tide, the Reaping, Refresh the Altar, Upkeep.

**Exalt.** An action: play a Host card from your hand into your Choir. It costs no Glory. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards to make room.

**Facedown.** A Host card turned face down. In a Choir it is inert, with no ability, no Offering value, and no contribution to Alignment. On the Altar it is hidden but active: its Offering still counts toward the Tide, and it is revealed as the first step of each Evening. Every Offer is placed facedown.

**Faithful.** *(Hidden Roles)* The Light faction. Wins if the Balance ends in the Heights.

**Fallen.** *(Hidden Roles)* The Dark faction. Wins if the Balance ends in the Abyss.

**Fate.** *(Hidden Roles)* Your secret faction: Faithful, Fallen, or Forsaken. Drawn at setup and revealed at game end.

**First Fruits.** The third step of Morning: every player reveals 1 card from hand at the same time, face up on the Altar, then draws 1. It earns no Glory and is not an Offer.

**Flip.** To turn a card Facedown or face up. A kind of Targeting when done to another player's card.

**Forsaken.** *(Hidden Roles)* The neutral faction. Wins if the Balance ends in the Divide. Cannot Turn.

**Gather.** An action: take 1 Host card from the Altar into your Choir. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards to make room.

**Glory.** The game's points and its currency. Earned through Offers, the Reaping and card abilities; spent on some abilities (and the Turn in Hidden Roles). Drawn from and paid into the Pot. Cannot drop below 0.

**Hand limit.** The number of cards you draw up to at Upkeep. Normally 3; the Dominion's is 5.

**Heights.** *(Hidden Roles)* The four spaces at the light end of the Balance. If the marker finishes here, the Faithful control the world.

**Host card.** The primary card type. Each has an ability and an Offering value. The deck has 69 cards: 23 Light, 23 Dark, and 23 Neutral or both sides.

**In the Morning.** A cadence. The ability works during Morning, at the step the card names.

**Light.** One of the two opposed Offering types, paired against Darkness. Light Offerings push the Tide toward the Light, and Light Host cards give your Choir a Light Alignment.

**Look at.** To see a hidden card or Fate privately. A kind of Targeting when done to another player's cards.

**Morning.** The first phase of a Day: the Event (Advanced game), Predictions, and First Fruits.

**Noon.** The second phase of a Day: each player takes one turn, clockwise from the first player.

**Neutral.** An Offering or Alignment value of zero. Neutral cards never move the Tide and never contribute to Alignment, but they still fill slots and may carry abilities.

**Offer.** An action: play 1 Host card from your hand facedown onto the Altar, then gain 1 Glory (the Offer's reward). An Offer is provisional, and any player may Gather it away before the Day ends.

**Offering value.** The Light, Dark, or Neutral strength printed on a Host card. It affects the Tide while on the Altar and Alignment while in a Choir.

**Pot.** The shared Glory supply that players draw Glory from and pay it into.

**Reaping, the.** The Evening scoring step. Every Choir is scored: alone with the Tide gains 3; two with it gain 1 each; three or more gain nothing. Alone against the Tide loses 3; otherwise each Choir against it loses 1. Then *At the end of the Day* abilities resolve.

**Refresh the Altar.** The Evening step that discards all remaining Altar cards.

**Replace.** To remove a card from a Choir and put another in its place. A kind of Targeting when done to another player's Choir.

**Reveal.** To show a hidden card or Fate to the whole table. A kind of Targeting when done to another player's cards.

**Set the Tide.** The Evening step that totals the Altar's Light against its Darkness to determine the Tide and its winning margin. *(Hidden Roles)* The Balance marker then moves by margin, up to 2 spaces.

**Target.** An ability Targets when it is aimed at another player or at their cards. The six kinds of Targeting are Look at, Reveal, Copy, Flip, Replace, and Discard. Cherubim protects your Choir from being Replaced or Discarded, Tribute charges a player 1 Glory to Target it, and Mirage protects the cards you placed on the Altar.

**Tide.** The Day's prevailing direction: Light, Dark, or Neutral. It is set each Evening by the Altar's totals and governs Reaping scoring.

**Turn (action).** *(Hidden Roles)* Once per game, spend your action and 1 Glory to defect to the opposing faction by placing a Turn card in your Choir. The Forsaken cannot Turn. Your new Fate takes effect at the next Morning, and a Turn on the final Day does not resolve in time.

**Turn card.** *(Hidden Roles)* The blank, permanent card placed when you Turn. It is not a Host card. It has no Offering value, no Alignment, and no ability, and it cannot be Targeted, replaced, or counted by any effect that references Host cards.

**Unclaimed Divide.** *(Hidden Roles)* If the game ends in the Divide but no Forsaken token was drawn at setup, no faction controls the world. The highest total Glory wins outright, regardless of Fate.

**Upkeep.** The final Evening step. Each player draws back up to their hand limit, then the starting player marker passes left. If the Host deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck.

**Spent card.** A card whose ability ends "then discard this card". Using it discards it.
