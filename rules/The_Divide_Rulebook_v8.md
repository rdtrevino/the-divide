# **THE DIVIDE: Rulebook**

*A game of divine ambition, open rivalry, and hidden loyalties.*

**Created by:** Robert Treviño
**Version:** Rulebook v8, matched to card data V38 (November 16, 2026)
**Players:** 3 to 6 (Open Game at 3 or 4, Hidden Roles at 5 or 6) | **Play Time:** 30 to 60 minutes
**Mechanisms:** Tableau Building, Negotiation, Variable Player Powers; Hidden Allegiance, Social Deduction, and Tug-of-War in Hidden Roles

---

## **What This Game Is**

Before the first dawn, the unfinished world rests on the Divide, the narrow span between the Heights above and the Abyss below. You are a Celestial, a primordial power competing for Glory. Day after Day, you and your rivals will feed a shared Altar, much of it in secret, and each night its weight sets the Tide: Light, Dark, or Neutral.

Your Choir, a row of three cards in front of you, leans toward the Light or the Dark. When the Tide runs your way, you gain Glory. When it runs against you, you lose it. Every Choir is public, so your rivals know exactly which Tide you need, and they will fight you for it.

**The first Evening that ends with any player at 10 Glory or more ends the game. The most Glory wins.**

---

## **Two Ways to Play**

The Divide has two modes built on the same cards and the same Day. This rulebook teaches the **Open Game** first. **Hidden Roles** adds secret factions and a tug-of-war track on top of it, and has its own section near the end.

| Players | Mode |
| ----- | ----- |
| 3 or 4 | Open Game |
| 5 or 6 | Hidden Roles |

---

## **The Things You Touch**

**The Altar.** A shared pool of cards in the center of the table. The Seed and First Fruits sit face up; every Offer goes on facedown, so no one is sure what the Altar holds until the Evening reveals it. At the end of each Day the combined Light and Darkness of every card on it set the Tide. The Altar is emptied every night, and any card on it, hidden or not, can be taken by another player before the Day ends.

**Your Choir.** Your personal row of 3 face-up cards, dealt full at setup. Its cards give you abilities, and each night the Choir is scored against the Tide: lean with it and you gain Glory, lean against it and you lose it.

**Your Hand.** Your hidden Host cards, normally 3, refilled each night. Your First Fruits come from it each Morning, and your facedown Offers keep its secrets a little longer.

**Glory.** Both score and currency. You earn it from your Offers and by scoring your Choir, you spend it to Exalt, and you may give it to other players to seal a deal. Glory comes from and returns to a shared supply called the Pot. Glory never drops below 0.

### **Reading a Host Card**

The Host deck is the game's one deck of playable cards. Every Host card shows:

* A **name** and an **ability**. Most abilities begin with a cadence that tells you when they work: *On your turn*, *In the Evening*, *At the Reaping*, *Once per Day*, *Once per game*, and so on. An ability with no cadence is always in effect.
* An **Offering value**: Light +1 or +2, Dark +1 or +2, or Neutral. A card's Offering pushes the Tide while it sits on the Altar and shapes your Choir's lean while it sits in your Choir. Neutral cards push nothing and lean nowhere. A +2 Offering appears only on once-per-game cards, and a spent once-per-game card keeps its +2 value (see Used Card in the Glossary).

## **Components**

* **72 Host cards:** 24 Light, 24 Dark, and 24 Neutral or Variable. The Open Game uses 66 of them (see Setup).
* **9 Celestial cards:** unique player powers.
* **20 Event cards:** one revealed each Day, changing that Day's rules. The Open Game uses 17 of them.
* **Glory markers** and **1 starting player marker.**
* **For Hidden Roles only:** the Balance (an 11-space track with 1 marker), Fate tokens (Faithful, Fallen, Forsaken), and Turn cards.

## **Setup**

1. **Prepare the decks.** Set these cards aside; they are used only in Hidden Roles:
   * **Host cards:** Revelation, Knowledge, Bounty, Flaming Sword, Belial, and Hubris. This leaves 66 Host cards.
   * **Event cards:** The Sabbath, Equilibrium, and Armageddon. This leaves 17 Event cards.
2. **Deal Celestials.** Each player receives a Celestial card, face up. These powers are public knowledge.
3. **Deal Host cards.** Each player takes a hand of 3 Host cards, kept hidden. Then deal each player 3 more Host cards face up in a row in front of them. This is their starting Choir. (The Dominion takes a hand of 4.)
4. **Finish.** Shuffle the Event deck, set all Glory to 0, and give a random player the starting player marker.

## **How a Day Works**

The game is played in rounds called **Days**. In the Open Game, play continues until a player reaches 10 Glory (see Ending the Game); Hidden Roles lasts 6 Days. Each Day has two phases. In the **Morning**, the Day's rules are revealed and every player takes one turn. In the **Evening**, the Altar is tallied, the Tide is set, and every Choir is scored.

### **Morning: The Unveiling**

Every Morning opens with a fixed four-step sequence, resolved before anyone takes a turn:

1. **The Event.** The first player draws the top Event card, reads it aloud, and places it face up. It changes the rules for the entire Day and applies to everyone equally. (If the Oracle is in play, they may first look at the card and choose to play it or bury it.)
2. **Declarations.** Any required predictions are locked in now, before the Seed is revealed. The Whisper makes their public call, a player with Prophecy declares, and if Casting Lots is the Event, every player commits a secret prediction.
3. **The Seed.** Flip the top card of the Host deck onto the Altar. This is the Seed, the Day's starting offering.
4. **First Fruits.** Every player secretly chooses 1 card from their hand. When everyone is ready, all reveal at once and place their card face up on the Altar, then each player draws 1 card. First Fruits is a tithe, not an Offer: it earns no Glory and does not trigger any ability that says "when you Offer."

   > *Example: Ada holds Michael, Moloch, and Wisdom. Her Choir leans Light, so she would like to give Michael, but Michael is a card she may want to Exalt later. She gives Wisdom (Light +1) instead, sees that Ben and Cy both gave Dark cards, and now knows the Altar starts the Day tilted toward the Dark.*

### **Morning: Your Turn**

Starting with the first player and moving clockwise, each player takes exactly one turn: choose one action, then activate your Choir.

**Offer.** Play 1 Host card from your hand **facedown** onto the Altar, then take your reward: gain 1 Glory, or draw 1 card. This is your income and your secret vote: the card's Offering pulls the Tide even while hidden, and the reward is yours to keep even if the card is later taken. You may look at your own facedown cards at any time. An Offer is provisional. Anyone acting after you can Gather it away before the Day ends.

> *Example: the Altar shows a Dark Seed and two Dark First Fruits. Ben leans Light. He Offers Moloch facedown, a Dark card, and takes 1 Glory. The table sees only that Ben Offered something. Cy, who leans Dark, has to decide whether that hidden card is help or a trap.*

**Gather.** Take 1 Host card from the Altar, or from the top of the Host deck, into your Choir. You may Gather a facedown card without looking at it first; it enters your Choir face up. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards to make room. This is how you reshape your Choir for free, and how you play defense: taking a Dark card off the Altar weakens the Dark Tide. But the card you take now shapes your own lean, and a raid that flips the Tide can leave you holding the wrong card at scoring.

**Exalt.** Pay 1 Glory to play a Host card from your hand into your Choir. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards to make room. This is the only way to move a card from your hidden hand into your Choir without it ever touching the public Altar. You pay for the privacy.

**Then activate.** After your action, you may activate each Host card in your Choir whose ability works on your turn. Each card activates at most once per turn, and only in its printed cadence.

### **Evening**

When every player has taken a turn, resolve the Evening in this exact order:

1. **Reveal the Altar.** Turn every facedown card on the Altar face up.

2. **In the Evening.** Resolve every ability marked *In the Evening*. These happen after the reveal but before the Altar is counted, so they see every card but not the Tide.

3. **Set the Tide.** Total all Light values on the Altar, total all Darkness values, and compare. The heavier side is the **Tide**. Its winning margin is the difference between the two totals. A tie, or an empty Altar, means a Neutral Tide.

   > *Example: the Altar holds Light +2, Light +1, Dark +1, Dark +1, and a Neutral card. Light totals 3, Darkness totals 2. The Tide is Light, with a winning margin of 1.*

4. **The Reaping.** Every player scores their Choir. Find your **Alignment**: the net difference between the Light and Darkness values in your Choir (Neutral cards count nothing). If your Alignment matches the Tide's direction, gain Glory equal to your net value. If it opposes the Tide, lose that much (never below 0). A net-zero Choir gains and loses nothing, and a Neutral Tide scores nothing for anyone. Gains and losses from Alignment are capped at 3 Glory per Reaping, even after doubling effects. Abilities marked *At the Reaping* resolve during this step, after the Tide is known.

   > *Example: your Choir holds Free Will (Light +2), Confession (Light +1), and Moloch (Dark +1). Your net Alignment is Light 2. If the Tide is Light, you gain 2 Glory. If the Tide is Dark, you lose 2. The Tide's margin does not matter here, only its direction.*

5. **Refresh the Altar.** Discard every card remaining on the Altar. Nothing carries over unless a card says otherwise.

6. **Upkeep.** Everyone draws back up to their hand limit, normally 3 (or discards down to it), and the starting player marker passes left. If the Host deck is ever empty, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck.

Then a new Day begins.

## **Deals and Table Talk**

Open negotiation is permitted at all times, and the game expects you to use it.

* **Glory moves freely.** You may give Glory to any player at any time, for any reason. Once given, it cannot be taken back. A gift can push someone to 10 Glory and end the game, so the closer everyone gets, the more every gift is worth.
* **Promises are not binding.** You may promise anything: future plays, safe passage, a Dark card held back. Betrayal is a legal, legitimate strategy. Trust accordingly.
* **Cards never change hands by agreement.** Host cards move only through explicit card actions and Celestial abilities. You cannot trade, gift, or sell them.

---

## **Ending the Game**

**The race to 10.** At the end of every Reaping, check Glory. If any player has **10 or more**, this was the final Day: the game ends after the Reaping, before the Altar is Refreshed. Otherwise a new Day begins. There is no Day limit, and every player always takes the same number of turns.

Then each player gains 1 bonus Glory for every Host card in their hand and Choir whose Offering matches the direction of the **final Day's Tide**. If the final Tide is Neutral, no bonus is awarded. The bonus can change the winner: reaching 10 first ends the game, but it does not win it.

> *Example: after the fourth Reaping, Ada has 10 Glory, Ben 9, and Cy 6. The game ends. The final Tide was Dark. Ada has 1 Dark card in her hand and Choir, Ben has 3. Ada finishes with 11 and Ben with 12, so Ben wins.*

**The most Glory wins.** If players tie, the tied player with more cards matching the final Tide wins. If they are still tied, they share the dawn.

## **The Heart of the Game**

Your Choir tells your rivals exactly which Tide you need, so expect them to fight you for it: a well-timed Offer against your lean, a Gather that strips your card from the Altar, a Dark hand held back for the Day your Choir commits to the Light. You score by riding the Tide, but you only control a share of it. Every card you put on the Altar helps whoever leans the same way, rival or not.

The facedown Altar is where the bluffing lives. A player who Offers against their own lean may be sabotaging a rival, setting a Snare, or simply lying about which way they will Gather. First Fruits shows you one honest card from every hand each Morning; everything after that has to be read from behavior.

No one knows which Day is the last until someone gets close. Once a player nears 10, every Day might be the final one, and the last Tide sets both the last Reaping and the endgame bonus. That is the moment to hold matching cards, to bring the leader down, or to push the game to an end while you are ahead.

At three players the Altar is small, and single cards swing the Tide hard. If your table finds the Tides too thin, try revealing **2 Seed cards** at each Unveiling instead of 1.

---

## **Advice for Your First Game**

Your starting Choir is dealt at random, so early leans are luck, not strategy. The honest signal is what a player discards: a card thrown out of a Choir tells you which way that player has decided to lean. Spend the early Days reshaping the Choir you were dealt, and lean harder on Offers later, when each card on the Altar swings a Tide that matters more. Watch the turn order, too. The last players to act each Day see the most of the Altar and have enormous power over where the Tide falls, though with facedown Offers they see less of it than they think. Count who Offered what, and remember that a player's First Fruits is the one card they had to show you.

## **Hidden Roles**

*For 5 or 6 players.*

In Hidden Roles, the world itself is at stake. Every player secretly belongs to a faction, and the Tide no longer only scores your Choir: it moves a marker across the Balance, and the marker's final position decides which faction wins.

**To win, you need two things:**

1. When the game ends, the world must belong to your secret faction.
2. Among the players of that faction, you must hold the most Glory.

Faction victory makes you eligible. Glory makes you the winner. You must help your side win the world while out-earning the allies helping you do it.

### **What Changes**

All Open Game rules apply, with these changes:

* **Use the full decks:** all 69 Host cards and all 20 Event cards.
* **Add the Balance, Fate tokens, and Turn cards.**
* **The Tide moves the Balance.** After Set the Tide, the marker moves toward the Tide: 1 space for a margin of exactly 1, or 2 spaces for a margin of 2 or more (2 is the cap). A Neutral Tide does not move it.
* **Turn** becomes a fourth action choice (see The Turn below).
* **Glory may be given on every Day,** including the final one.
* **The game can end early,** and the winner is decided differently (see Ending a Hidden Roles Game).

### **The Balance**

An 11-space track in the center of the table with a single marker on it. The middle 3 spaces are the **Divide**. The 4 spaces on each end are the **Heights** (Light) and the **Abyss** (Dark).

### **Additional Setup**

Before dealing Celestials:

1. **Draw secret Fates.** Pool Fate tokens by player count using the table below, mix them in the bag, and have each player secretly draw one. Leave the rest in the bag, unrevealed. Because leftover tokens stay hidden, no one knows the true headcount of any faction. There may not even be a Forsaken at the table.

| Player Count | Faithful | Fallen | Forsaken | Left in Bag |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 4 Players | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 5 Players | 3 | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| 6 Players | 3 | 3 | 1 | 1 |

2. **Place the Balance** in the center with the marker on the middle space of the Divide.
3. **Set out the Turn cards** face up beside the Altar: 1 card for 4 players, 2 cards for 5 or 6 players.

### **The Factions**

* **Faithful** win if the marker ends in the Heights.
* **Fallen** win if the marker ends in the Abyss.
* **Forsaken** win if the marker ends in the Divide. The Forsaken cannot Turn; they are bound to the center.

### **The Turn: Defection**

Once per game, during any Day except the last, a player may spend their action to **Turn**, defecting to the opposing faction (Faithful to Fallen, or Fallen to Faithful).

**To defect:** pay 1 Glory and place one of the face-up Turn cards from beside the Altar into your Choir. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards to clear the space.

**The Turn card is a scar, not a tool.** It is not a Host card. It has no Offering, no Alignment, and no ability. It cannot be Targeted, discarded, replaced, or counted by any effect that references Host cards, and it occupies its Choir slot for the rest of the game.

**Defection is not instant.** You remain your original Fate until the end of the current Day; your new Fate takes hold at the next Unveiling. A Turn taken on the final Day fails to resolve in time, and you score as your original Fate.

**Defections are scarce.** The supply of Turn cards is the limit: 1 in 4-player games, 2 in games of 5 or 6.

### **Ending a Hidden Roles Game**

The game ends **immediately** if the Balance marker reaches the final space of the Heights or the Abyss. Otherwise it ends after the Evening of the 6th Day.

The marker's final position decides who controls the world: the Heights for the Faithful, the Abyss for the Fallen, the Divide for the Forsaken.

**Then all Fates are revealed**, and each player gains 1 bonus Glory for every Host card in their hand and Choir that matches the winning faction's alignment.

**The winner** is the player with the most Glory among those whose Fate matches the controlling faction. Everyone else, however rich, has backed the wrong world.

**The Unclaimed Divide.** If the marker finishes in the Divide but the Forsaken token was never drawn at setup, no faction controls the world. The restriction lifts: the highest Glory total at the table wins outright, regardless of Fate. No endgame card bonus is awarded in this scenario.

### **The Heart of Hidden Roles**

Your secret Fate wants the Balance in one place. Your Glory wants your Choir aligned with wherever the Tide is actually going. These two goals regularly pull apart.

A Fallen player might spend a Day offering Light because their Choir leans Light and they need the points. A Faithful player might sandbag a round to avoid revealing their allegiance. The Forsaken profits most when the other factions cancel each other out. Every card you play gives the table information, and controlling how that information is read is where games are won. Don't panic about hiding your Fate in the first two Days: the Reaping rewards riding the Tide no matter your allegiance, so playing out of character is often just good play.

### **Still to Be Decided for Hidden Roles**

These questions are open and will be settled before the next Hidden Roles playtest:

* A tiebreak for the winner within the winning faction.
* How the endgame card bonus works when the Forsaken win.
* Whether a player who Turns reveals their Fate.
* The printed Fate token counts (the current print run has 4 Faithful, 4 Fallen, and 2 Forsaken; setup never needs more than 3, 3, and 1).

## **The Nine Celestials**

Each player commands one Celestial, a public, asymmetric power. A Celestial power never uses your turn action. It works only in its listed timing window, and powers written as standing rules (such as the Dominion's hand limit) are always in effect.

| Celestial | Power |
| ----- | ----- |
| **I. The Tempest** | Once per Day, destroy one Host card from the Altar. |
| **II. The Oracle** | Once per Day, during the Unveiling, look at the top Event card. Play it, or bury it at the bottom of the deck. |
| **III. The Eclipse** | In the Evening, before the Altar is revealed, once per Day, treat one Host card in your Choir as Neutral for this Day's Reaping. |
| **IV. The Cantor** | During your turn, you can Offer 2 cards to the Altar instead of 1, with a reward for each. |

| **VII. The Dominion** | Your hand limit is 4 instead of 3. Begin the game with 4 cards in hand. |

| **IX. The Arbiter** | At the Reaping, if you gain 2 or more Glory, take 1 of it from a player of your choice. If you lose Glory, give 1 of it to the player, other than you, with the least Glory. |

## **Rulings**

These clarify how specific cards work. The card text is always the starting point; these rulings settle the questions it leaves open.

**Nephilim.** Counts as both Light +1 and Dark +1 for any card that checks for a Light card or a Dark card, so on its own it satisfies Twilight. For your Alignment and the Tide its two values are Neutral (0), even beside Uriel or Beelzebub, and it never earns the endgame bonus.

**Shroud.** Its value on any Day is the opposite of the previous Day's Tide, both on the Altar and in your Choir. At the end of the game it keeps the value it had on the final Day, and Shroud earns the endgame bonus only if that value matches the final Tide.

**The Whisper.** A correct prediction means your Choir gains its net value instead of losing it, subject to the usual cap of 3. A Choir with net Alignment 0 gains nothing.

**The Eclipse.** Chosen in the Evening, just before the Altar is revealed, so you choose without seeing the hidden Offerings. The chosen card is Neutral for that Day's Reaping only.

**The Arbiter.** Applies only to the Glory gained or lost from your Choir's Alignment at the Reaping. A gain of 1 comes entirely from the Pot. If no other player has Glory, the whole gain comes from the Pot.

**Mammon.** If no other player has Glory to take, the ability yields nothing. Its Exalt payment always goes to the player, other than you, with the least Glory.

**Leviathan.** "Your Alignment did not gain you Glory" covers a Neutral Tide, a net-zero Choir, and a Reaping loss. Glory from other At the Reaping abilities does not count either way.

**Apple.** It compares your Choir's Alignment at this Reaping with the previous Day's Tide. On the first Day, or after a Neutral Tide, it pays nothing. Its Glory is not Alignment scoring, so the cap of 3 does not apply to it, and it pays whether your Choir gains or loses at this Reaping.

**The most or least Glory.** When a card names the player with the most or the least Glory and several players are tied, the player using the card chooses among them, unless the card says otherwise (Envy hits every tied leader).

**Heresy.** You decide whether to reverse your scoring after the Tide is set. Choosing to reverse is an activation: it costs 1 Glory on a Tithe Day and can be negated by Silence.

**Raguel.** Only the Raguel holder's Reaping loss is shared. The Bound player loses 1 Glory and the holder loses 1 less (so a loss of 1 costs the holder nothing). A Bound player who is already losing Glory at that Reaping loses the larger amount, not both. If the Bound player loses Glory, the holder is unaffected.

**Tithe.** Every Host ability you activate costs 1 Glory, whether on your turn, in the Evening, or at the Reaping. Always-on abilities (such as Cherubim, Veil, Tribute, and Mirage) are never activated and cost nothing. If you cannot pay, you cannot activate.

**Silence.** When a player announces an ability, the holder of an unused Silence may negate it before it takes effect. Any cost already paid, including Tithe, is lost.

**Facedown Offerings.** Every Offer goes on the Altar facedown, including both of the Cantor's. Hymn and Lamentation are the exception: when you use them to Offer two cards, both go face up so everyone can see they share a color, and you take one reward. Seeds, First Fruits, and cards added by other abilities follow their own text.

**Mirage.** Protects only your own facedown Offerings, and only until the Evening reveal. Ithuriel, Urim, Asmadai, Abaddon, and the Tempest cannot touch them. A protected card can still be Gathered. At the Reaping you gain 1 Glory (only 1, however many qualify) if a card you Offered facedown that Day is still on the Altar and its color matches the Tide. First Fruits, Vessel's card, and the Seed do not count.

**Raphael and Azazel.** To destroy, you turn your own Offering face up and give up its reward. Only face-up cards can be destroyed, so a facedown card is safe from them.

**Ithuriel, Urim, and Asmadai.** Each acts only on facedown cards. Asmadai's exchange swaps the card's identity but not its owner: the card stays facedown and still counts as Offered by the player who placed it. Urim's look is private; you may tell the table what you saw, truthfully or not.

**Snare.** Pays only when someone Gathers a card you Offered. First Fruits, Seeds, and Vessel's card never trigger it.

**Michael.** The Flipped card is Facedown (inert: no ability, no Offering value) until the Reaping ends, then it turns face up by itself. Before then its owner may pay 1 Glory at any time to Flip it face up, including during the Reaping after the Tide is set and before scoring. If the game ends at that Reaping, the card is face up again in time for the endgame bonus.

**Uriel and Beelzebub.** Each Reaping, after the Tide is set and before scoring, decide whether to pay 1 Glory. Paying is an activation (Tithe, Silence). If you hold both, each needs its own payment.

**Morning Star.** Used at the Reaping, after the Tide is set and before scoring, for 1 Glory. The chosen card flips color for that Reaping only; Nephilim and Neutral cards cannot be chosen.

**Scapegoat.** Name your Scapegoat on your turn. It covers only the Reaping of that same Day; if you lose nothing that Day, the protection is gone.

**Vessel.** Its card goes on the Altar facedown but is not an Offer. You still take the Offer reward (1 Glory or 1 card), but no Offer abilities trigger (Mammon, Raphael, Azazel, Hymn, Lamentation, Snare, Mirage), and Asmadai treats it as nobody's Offering.

**Urim.** Look at up to 3 facedown cards that you did not place. What you say about them is up to you.

**Eternity.** Takes any face-up card on the Altar (all are revealed by then) that is not protected by Sanctuary, before the Tide is set, so it also changes the Tide.

**Outcast.** The Reaping bonus is checked after Alignment scoring, against everyone's Glory at that moment.

**Prophecy.** A Neutral Tide counts as a miss: you lose 1.

**Providence.** Costs 1 Glory to use. Paying is part of the activation.

**Council.** The holder swaps first, and may not swap out Council itself. Then each other player may pay the holder 1 Glory to make one swap of their own. A player who cannot spend Glory (for example during the Fast) cannot join. Swapping your own cards is not Targeting.

**Tree of Life and Forger.** Tree of Life takes only the top card of the discard pile, into your hand. Forger, when you Gather, may take any Host card from the discard pile, into your Choir.

**Envy.** Usable only while the player with the most Glory has at least 3 more Glory than you, so you can never be one of the leaders it hits.

**Protection.** Cherubim and Veil divide protection between them. Cherubim stops other players from Replacing or Discarding your cards. Veil stops them from Looking at, Revealing, Copying, or Flipping your cards. Tribute and Lilith respond to any Targeting at all. Tribute's 1 Glory is paid to the Pot, not to Tribute's holder.

## **Glossary**

**Abyss.** *(Hidden Roles)* The four spaces at the dark end of the Balance. If the marker finishes here, the Fallen control the world.

**Activate.** To use a Host ability you choose to use, on your turn, in the Evening, or at the Reaping. After your action, you may activate each card in your Choir whose ability works on your turn, once per turn. Always-on abilities are never activated.

**Alignment.** The net difference between the Light and Darkness values of the Host cards in your Choir. A net toward Light is a Light Alignment, a net toward Dark is a Dark Alignment, and an equal split is Neutral (net zero). Scored during the Reaping.

**Altar.** The shared central pool of Host cards. Cards arrive via the Seed, First Fruits, and Offers, and leave via Gathers. The Altar's contents set the Tide each Evening and are then discarded.

**At the Reaping.** A cadence. The ability resolves during the Reaping, after the Tide is set and known.

**Balance, the.** *(Hidden Roles)* The 11-space tug-of-war track. The center 3 spaces are the Divide. The 4 spaces at each end are the Heights and the Abyss.

**Celestial.** The unique, public power each player commands. A Celestial power does not use your turn action and works only in its listed timing window.

**Choir.** Your row of up to 3 face-up Host cards, dealt full at setup. Its Alignment is scored each Reaping, and its cards may be activated.

**Copy.** To use the ability of another player's card as if it were yours. A kind of Targeting.

**Darkness.** One of the two opposed Offering types, paired against Light. Dark Offerings push the Tide toward the Dark, and Dark Host cards give your Choir a Dark Alignment.

**Day.** A full round of play, split into Morning and Evening. The Open Game continues until a player ends a Day with 10 Glory or more; Hidden Roles lasts 6 Days.

**Declarations.** The second step of the Unveiling, when the Whisper, Prophecy, and Casting Lots predictions are locked in before the Seed is revealed.

**Discard.** To move a card to the discard pile. When another player's ability discards one of your cards, it is a kind of Targeting.

**Divide.** The center 3 spaces of the Balance. *(Hidden Roles)* If the marker finishes here, the Forsaken control the world.

**Evening.** The second phase of a Day. In order: In the Evening abilities, Set the Tide, the Reaping, Refresh the Altar, Upkeep.

**Exalt.** An action: pay 1 Glory to play a Host card from your hand into your Choir. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards to make room.

**Facedown.** A Host card turned face down. In a Choir it is inert, with no ability, no Offering value, and no contribution to Alignment. On the Altar it is hidden but active: its Offering still counts toward the Tide, and it is revealed as the first step of each Evening. Every Offer is placed facedown.

**First Fruits.** The fourth step of the Unveiling: every player reveals 1 card from hand at the same time, face up on the Altar, then draws 1. It earns no Glory and is not an Offer.

**Faithful.** *(Hidden Roles)* The Light faction. Wins if the Balance ends in the Heights.

**Fallen.** *(Hidden Roles)* The Dark faction. Wins if the Balance ends in the Abyss.

**Fate.** *(Hidden Roles)* Your secret faction: Faithful, Fallen, or Forsaken. Drawn at setup and revealed at game end.

**Flip.** To turn a card Facedown or face up. A kind of Targeting when done to another player's card.

**Forsaken.** *(Hidden Roles)* The neutral faction. Wins if the Balance ends in the Divide. Cannot Turn.

**Gather.** An action: take 1 Host card from the Altar or the top of the Host deck into your Choir. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards to make room.

**Glory.** The game's points and its currency. Earned through Offer rewards and scoring, spent on Exalt (and the Turn in Hidden Roles), and transferable to other players. Drawn from and paid into the Pot. Cannot drop below 0.

**Hand limit.** The number of cards you draw up to at Upkeep. Normally 3; the Dominion's is 4.

**Heights.** *(Hidden Roles)* The four spaces at the light end of the Balance. If the marker finishes here, the Faithful control the world.

**Host card.** The primary card type. Each has an ability and an Offering value. The full deck has 72 cards: 24 Light, 24 Dark, and 24 Neutral or Variable. The Open Game uses 66.

**In the Evening.** A cadence. The ability resolves at the start of the Evening, before the Altar is counted and the Tide is set.

**Light.** One of the two opposed Offering types, paired against Darkness. Light Offerings push the Tide toward the Light, and Light Host cards give your Choir a Light Alignment.

**Look at.** To see a hidden card or Fate privately. A kind of Targeting when done to another player's cards.

**Morning.** The first phase of a Day: the Unveiling, then each player takes one turn clockwise.

**Neutral.** An Offering or Alignment value of zero. Neutral cards never move the Tide and never contribute to Alignment, but they still fill slots and may carry abilities.

**Offer.** An action: play 1 Host card from your hand facedown onto the Altar, then gain 1 Glory or draw 1 card. An Offer is provisional, and any player may Gather it away before the Day ends.

**Offering value.** The Light, Dark, or Neutral strength printed on a Host card. It affects the Tide while on the Altar and Alignment while in a Choir.

**Pot.** The shared Glory supply that players draw Glory from and pay it into.

**Reaping, the.** The Evening scoring step. Compare your Choir's Alignment to the Tide. A match gains Glory equal to your net Alignment, opposition loses that much, and a Neutral Choir or a Neutral Tide scores nothing. Gains and losses from Alignment are capped at 3 Glory per Reaping.

**Refresh the Altar.** The Evening step that discards all remaining Altar cards.

**Replace.** To remove a card from a Choir and put another in its place. A kind of Targeting when done to another player's Choir.

**Reveal.** To show a hidden card or Fate to the whole table. A kind of Targeting when done to another player's cards.

**Seed.** The Host card revealed onto the Altar during the Unveiling to start the Day's pool.

**Set the Tide.** The Evening step that totals the Altar's Light against its Darkness to determine the Tide and its winning margin. *(Hidden Roles)* The Balance marker then moves by margin, up to 2 spaces.

**Target.** An ability Targets when it is aimed at another player or at their cards. The six kinds of Targeting are Look at, Reveal, Copy, Flip, Replace, and Discard. Some cards protect against all Targeting (Tribute, Lilith) and some against specific kinds (Cherubim, Veil).

**Tide.** The Day's prevailing direction: Light, Dark, or Neutral. It is set each Evening by the Altar's totals and governs Reaping scoring.

**Turn (action).** *(Hidden Roles)* Once per game, spend your action and 1 Glory to defect to the opposing faction by placing a Turn card in your Choir. The Forsaken cannot Turn. Your new Fate takes effect at the next Unveiling, and a Turn on the final Day does not resolve in time.

**Turn card.** *(Hidden Roles)* The blank, permanent card placed when you Turn. It is not a Host card. It has no Offering value, no Alignment, and no ability, and it cannot be Targeted, replaced, or counted by any effect that references Host cards.

**Unclaimed Divide.** *(Hidden Roles)* If the game ends in the Divide but no Forsaken token was drawn at setup, no faction controls the world. The highest total Glory wins outright, regardless of Fate.

**Unveiling, the.** The fixed opening sequence of each Morning, resolved before any turns: (1) the Event, (2) Declarations, (3) the Seed, (4) First Fruits.

**Upkeep.** The final Evening step. Each player draws back up to their hand limit, then the starting player marker passes left. If the Host deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck.

**Used Card (Sideways).** A once-per-game card whose ability has been spent is turned sideways. It keeps its Offering value and Alignment but loses its ability, and stays in its slot until replaced or moved.
