# **THE DIVIDE: Rulebook**

*A game of divine ambition, shifting loyalties, and one shared Altar.*

**Created by:** Robert Treviño
**Version:** Rulebook v15, for card data V45
**Players:** 3 or 4 | **Play Time:** 25 to 35 minutes
**Mechanisms:** Hidden Allegiance, Public Goods, Engine Building, Negotiation, Bluffing

---

## **Overview**

Before the first dawn, the unfinished world rests on the Divide, the narrow span between the Heights above and the Abyss below. You are a power of Heaven or the Abyss, competing for **Glory**.

* **You never quite know who your side is.** Each player secretly holds six **allegiance tokens**, some Light and some Dark, in a mix no one else knows. Nobody is purely on one side.
* **Everyone feeds one shared Altar.** Each Day, players put cards on the Altar, most of them facedown. The heavier side, Light or Dark, **wins the Day**, and it is marked on the **Tide track**.
* **Your tokens pay at the end.** Every token is worth 1 Glory for each Day its side won.
* **Your Choir pays every Day.** Your Choir is a row of up to four face-up cards. Each Evening, every card in it of the winning color scores 2 Glory.
* **Help yourself or help your side.** Offering a card to the Altar pushes a side, but the card is gone from your hand. Building your Choir pays only you. You can even Offer one of your tokens: it is the strongest push there is, and it costs you your share in that side.
* **Seven Days, and a Judgment Day.** Every game lasts 7 Days. On the last, everyone chooses their action in secret before anyone acts. Then the tokens are revealed, and the most Glory wins.

## **Ways to Play**

| Game | Players | What it adds |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Base game** | 3 or 4 | The Host cards, the Altar, your Choir and the allegiance tokens. Start here. |
| **Advanced game** | 3 or 4 | **Celestials** (a public power for each player) and **Events** (a new rule each Day). |

Both use the same deck of 69 Host cards. *(Hidden Roles, the old mode for 5 and 6 players, was retired in V45: the allegiance tokens give every game the secret sides it was for.)*

## **What's in the Box**

* **69 Host cards:** 34 Light, 34 Dark, and Acolyte, whose color changes. Every card is Light or Dark.
* **24 allegiance tokens:** 12 Light and 12 Dark (use 9 of each with 3 players).
* **The Tide track:** 7 spaces, one per Day, and Day markers that are Light on one side and Dark on the other.
* **9 Celestial cards** and **17 Event cards** *(Advanced)*.
* **Glory markers** and **1 first player marker.**

### **The Places on the Table**

| Place | What it is |
| ----- | ----- |
| **The Altar** | A shared pool in the center. First Fruits sit face up; Offers go on facedown, so no one is sure what the Altar holds until the Evening. Each Evening the heavier side wins the Day. Anything on it can be taken by another player before the Day ends. |
| **The Tide track** | The record of which side won each Day. Your tokens score from it at the end. |
| **Your Choir** | Your row of up to 4 face-up cards; you start with 2. Its cards give you abilities, and each one scores 2 Glory on every Day its color wins. |
| **Your hand** | Your hidden Host cards, normally 3, refilled each Evening. |
| **Your tokens** | Your secret allegiance tokens, kept hidden until the end. Everyone can see how many you hold, not which. |
| **Glory** | Both score and currency, from a shared supply called the **Pot**. It never drops below 0. |

### **Reading a Host Card**

Every Host card shows:

* **A name and an ability.** An ability begins with when it works: *In the Morning*, *On your turn*, *In the Evening* (before the Altar is revealed), or *At the end of the Day*. An ability with no label is always on, or starts with "When…" and works whenever that happens. Some cards say "then discard this card": they are **spent** when used.
* **An Offering value:** Light +1 or Dark +1; Hymn and Lamentation are +2. On the Altar it pushes its side. In your Choir it is the card's color: a Light card scores on Light Days, a Dark card on Dark Days. Acolyte takes the color of the most recent Day won.

## **Setup**

1. **Shuffle the Host deck** (69 cards).
2. **Deal the allegiance tokens.** Use 9 Light and 9 Dark with 3 players, 12 and 12 with 4. Shuffle them facedown and deal **6 to each player**. Look at yours and keep them hidden. Mixes are uneven (5 and 1, 4 and 2, 3 and 3), and nobody knows anyone else's.
3. **Deal Host cards.** Each player takes a hidden hand of 3, then 2 more face up in a row in front of them: their starting Choir.
4. **Finish.** Set all Glory to 0, place the Tide track, and give a random player the first player marker.

**Advanced game:** also shuffle the 17 Events and deal each player a Celestial, face up. The Dominion takes a hand of 5.

## **A Day at a Glance**

Every game lasts **7 Days**. The seventh is **Judgment Day**: at Noon, everyone chooses their action in secret (see below).

| Phase | Step | What happens |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Morning** | 1. The Event *(Advanced)* | Reveal the Day's Event. Its rule applies to everyone, all Day. |
| | 2. Predictions | Abilities marked *In the Morning* are used now. |
| | 3. First Fruits | Everyone puts 1 card from hand facedown in the middle; shuffle them and turn them face up onto the Altar, then everyone draws 1. |
| **Noon** | Turns | Each player, clockwise from the first player: one action, then activate your Choir. |
| | Judgment Day *(Day 7 only)* | Everyone first chooses their action in secret; the turns then carry them out in order. |
| **Evening** | 1. Reveal the Altar | Abilities marked *In the Evening*, then shuffle the facedown Offers together and turn them face up. |
| | 2. The Day is won | The heavier side wins the Day: mark it on the Tide track. |
| | 3. The Reaping | Every Choir card of the winning color scores 2 Glory. Then *At the end of the Day* abilities. |
| | 4. Tokens left on the Altar | Handed out, one at a time, to whoever holds the fewest. |
| | 5. Refresh and Upkeep | Discard the Altar; draw (or discard) to your hand limit of 3; pass the first player marker left. |

## **Morning**

1. **The Event** *(Advanced game)*. The first player reveals the top Event card. It changes the rules for the whole Day.
2. **Predictions.** Abilities marked *In the Morning* are used now, before any card reaches the Altar.
3. **First Fruits.** Every player chooses 1 card from hand and puts it facedown in the middle. Shuffle them together and turn them face up onto the Altar, so no one knows whose is whose. Then everyone draws 1. First Fruits is not an Offer.

---

## **Noon: Your Turn**

Starting with the first player and moving clockwise, each player takes exactly one turn: **choose one action, then activate your Choir.**

| Action | What you do | What it does |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Offer** | Play 1 card from your hand **facedown** onto the Altar, **or one of your allegiance tokens**. | A hidden push for a side. A token counts **2** for its color, and it is gone from your hand of tokens. |
| **Gather** | Take 1 card from the Altar into your Choir, **or 1 token from the Altar** into your hidden tokens. | A new Choir card (discard one first if your Choir is full), and one push fewer on the Altar. A token needs no room. |
| **Exalt** | Play 1 card from your hand into your Choir. | Exactly the card you want, without it touching the Altar. If your Choir is full, discard one of its cards first. |

**Offer: the public good.** An Offer earns you nothing directly: it pushes the Day toward a side, and everyone who holds that side gains from it, whoever they are. The table sees that you Offered, and whether it was a card or a token, but not what. You may look at your own facedown cards at any time. Anyone acting after you can Gather your Offer away.

**Gather: take from the middle.** You may Gather a facedown card or token without looking at it first. Taking a card off the Altar weakens its side.

**Exalt: build exactly what you want.** It costs nothing but the card.

**Then activate.** After your action, you may activate each card in your Choir whose ability works *On your turn*, each at most once.

### **Judgment Day (Day 7)**

On the last Day, after First Fruits, **every player chooses their whole action in secret**: which action, and exactly which card or token (Offer *this* card, Exalt *that* one, Gather *that* card or token from the Altar). Write it down, or set the card facedown in front of you with a pointer.

Then play the turns in order as usual, starting with the first player. On your turn, carry out the action you chose, then use your *On your turn* abilities as normal. **If your chosen action can no longer be done** (the card you meant to Gather was taken first, an Offer is now blocked by Anathema, the card left your hand), **you do nothing** for your action.

No one acts last with the most knowledge on the Day that decides the game: everyone commits blind.

---

## **Evening**

1. **Reveal the Altar.** First, abilities marked *In the Evening* (Fig Leaves; in the Advanced game, the Eclipse). Then shuffle every facedown card and token on the Altar together and turn them face up, so no one learns whose Offer was whose.
2. **The Day is won.** Total the Light on the Altar against the Dark (each token counts 2 for its color). The heavier side **wins the Day**; mark it on the Tide track. A tie, or an empty Altar, is **Neutral**: no one wins the Day. The difference is the **winning margin**.
3. **The Reaping.** Every face-up Host card in every Choir **of the winning color scores 2 Glory** for its owner. Nothing is lost at the Reaping (Ordeal aside). On a Neutral Day, no Choir scores. Then resolve every ability marked *At the end of the Day*.
4. **Tokens left on the Altar.** Shuffle them facedown and hand them out one at a time, each to the player who holds the fewest tokens at that moment (ties: the first of them in turn order). Only the receiver looks.
5. **Refresh and Upkeep.** Discard every card on the Altar. Everyone draws back up to their hand limit (or discards down to it), and the first player marker passes left. If the Host deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck.

> *Example: Light wins the Day. Ada's Choir holds two Light cards and a Dark one: she scores 4. Ben's Choir holds only Dark cards: he scores nothing today, but his Dark cards will score on Dark Days.*

---

## **Ending the Game**

After the Evening of Day 7, everyone reveals their tokens.

1. **Tokens score.** Each token scores **1 Glory for every Day its color won** on the Tide track.
2. **The Miser's Rule.** The player who Offered the **fewest times** loses 3 Glory (all of them, if tied). If everyone Offered equally often, nobody does.
3. **The most Glory wins.** Players who tie share the win.

> *Example: Light won 4 Days and Dark won 2. Ada holds 4 Light and 2 Dark tokens: 4 × 4 + 2 × 2 = 20, added to the Glory her Choir earned.*

## **How to Play Well**

**Everyone is partly on your side.** Every player holds both colors. A Light Day pays every Light token at the table, yours and your rivals'. Who gets the most from a Day depends on who paid for it.

**An Offer is a gift to everyone who shares that side.** It helps your tokens, and everyone else's of the same color. The best players Offer when a Day hangs in the balance and build when it does not, and they make sure they are not the one who Offered least.

**A token is your strongest push and your most expensive one.** Offered, it counts double; but it is no longer yours, and if it is left on the Altar it goes to whoever holds the fewest.

**Read the table, not the cards.** First Fruits and the reveal are anonymous, so you see what was given, not who gave it. A Choir is public, but it is a claim, not a proof: a player full of Light cards may hold mostly Dark tokens.

**Talk.** Deals, threats and promises are open and never binding. The Tide track gives everyone the same numbers to argue over: "Light is up 3–1. Whoever holds Light should be paying now."

## **Advanced: The Nine Celestials**

In the Advanced game, each player commands one Celestial, a public power. A Celestial power never uses your turn action. Its timing works like a Host card's, each power is used at most once per Day, and powers written as standing rules (such as the Dominion's hand limit) are always in effect.

| Celestial | Power |
| ----- | ----- |
| **I. The Tempest** | When you Offer, you may destroy a card on the Altar, face up or facedown. |
| **II. The Oracle** | In the Morning: Look at the top 2 Event cards. Play one, and put the other on the bottom of the Event deck. |
| **III. The Eclipse** | In the Morning: Choose a Light or Dark card in your Choir. It counts as the other color this Day. |
| **IV. The Cantor** | When you Offer, draw a card. |
| **V. The Patron** | On your turn: Exchange a card from your hand with a card in another player's Choir. |
| **VI. The Echo** | On your turn: Use the On your turn ability of a card in another player's Choir, as if it were yours. Spent cards cannot be copied. |
| **VII. The Dominion** | Your hand limit is 5 instead of 3. Begin the game with 5 cards in hand. |
| **VIII. The Whisper** | In the Morning: Name Light or Dark aloud. If that side wins the Day, gain 1 Glory; if the other side wins, lose 1. |
| **IX. The Arbiter** | When your Choir scores at the Reaping, take 1 of that Glory from another player whose Choir scored less. |

## **Advanced: Event Cards**

In the Advanced game, one Event is revealed each Morning. It changes the rules for that whole Day and applies equally to all players.

| Event | Effect |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Creation** | In the Morning, reveal 3 Host cards from the deck onto the Altar. |
| **Manna** | Each player draws 1 card; hand limits increase by 1 this Day. Discard back to your limit in the Evening. |
| **Ascension** | Light cards on the Altar count double when the Tide is set. |
| **Let There Be Light** | The abilities of Dark Host cards in Choirs do not work this Day, including abilities that work on their own. The cards still score at the Reaping. Neutral cards are unaffected. |
| **Divine Calling** | When you Exalt this Day, gain 1 Glory. |
| **Dispersion** | Each player passes their entire hand to the player on their left. |
| **Purgatory** | This Day counts for neither side on the Tide track. Choirs still score. |
| **Casting Lots** | Each player secretly predicts the Tide in the Morning: Light, Dark, or Neutral. At the Reaping, a correct call gains 1 Glory (2 for Neutral); a wrong call loses 1. |
| **Pandemonium** | First Fruits are placed facedown, and no card on the Altar may be flipped or looked at before the Evening. |
| **False Prophecy** | Mark the Tide track as normal. At the Reaping, Choirs score as if the other side had won. |
| **Famine** | No Choir scores at the Reaping this Day. |
| **Babel** | No table talk or deals this Day. |
| **Fall From Grace** | Dark cards on the Altar count double when the Tide is set. |
| **The Plague** | Each player discards 1 Host card from their Choir and replaces it from the top of the deck. |
| **Tithe** | Pay 1 Glory for each Host ability you activate this Day. |
| **The Flood** | The abilities of Light Host cards in Choirs do not work this Day, including abilities that work on their own. The cards still score at the Reaping. Neutral cards are unaffected. |
| **The Fast** | No one may Exalt or spend Glory this Day. |

## **Rulings**

These settle questions the card text leaves open. The card text is always the starting point.

### **General**

**Tokens are not cards.** An allegiance token on the Altar counts 2 for its color, can be Gathered, and is shuffled into the reveal; no card ability flips, looks at, moves, protects or destroys it, and Ascension and Fall from Grace do not double it.

**Scored.** A Choir *scored* at the Reaping if it gained Glory there (at least one card of the winning color, and no Scapegoat). Mercy, Council, Favor, Defiance, Raguel, Bounty, Parasite and the Arbiter all ask this.

**Activating.** To activate is to use an ability you choose to use. Always-on abilities are never activated. On your turn, each Choir card activates at most once.

**The most or least Glory.** When a card names the player with the most or the least Glory and several are tied, the player using the card chooses among them, unless the card says otherwise (Envy hits every tied leader). Tokens do not count until the end.

**Spent cards.** A card that says "then discard this card" goes to the discard pile as soon as its ability is used (Michael, Free Will, Deliverance, Abaddon, Ordeal). Stores (Providence, Mammon) are discarded when you take their Glory; Glory on them is not yours until then.

**Exalt and Absolution.** The card being Exalted is set aside first, so Absolution's hand discard cannot take it.

**Tithe (Event).** Every Host ability you activate costs 1 Glory. Always-on abilities and Celestial powers cost nothing. If you cannot pay, you cannot activate.

**Purgatory (Event).** The Day is still won and Choirs still score, but the Day is not marked for either side on the Tide track, so it scores no tokens.

**False Prophecy (Event).** The Tide track records the side that really won; only the Choirs score the other way.

**The Flood and Let There Be Light.** Every ability of the named alignment is off for that Day, including always-on ones. The cards still score at the Reaping. On a Let There Be Light Day, Parasite may be discarded like any other card.

**Divine Calling (Event).** Pays for every Exalt into your own Choir, including one from the discard pile with Forger, but not for playing a card into another player's Choir.

**Silence.** The chosen player's Choir abilities stop working, always-on ones included, until the start of your next turn.

**Anathema and Flaming Sword.** From the end of your turn until the start of your next turn, no other player may Offer, cards or tokens (Anathema), or Exalt (Flaming Sword).

**Judgment Day and Free Will.** Free Will's second action is chosen on your turn as usual; only your first action was chosen in secret. A secret action that does nothing (it could no longer be done) is not an Offer.

**The Miser's Rule.** Every Offer action counts once, a card or a token. Fig Leaves taking a card back does not undo the Offer. First Fruits and Vessel's card are not Offers.

### **The Altar and Facedown Cards**

**Facedown Offerings.** Every Offer goes on the Altar facedown, except Hymn and Lamentation, which go face up.

**The anonymous reveal.** Before the Altar is revealed, the facedown cards and tokens are shuffled together, so the table learns what was Offered but not by whom. Abilities still know who placed what (Snare, Mirage, Vessel), and you may always say what you Offered, truthfully or not.

**Revelation, Knowledge, Belial, and Winnowing.** Each acts on facedown Altar cards, never on tokens. Revelation flips one face up for everyone, then may flip a face-up card facedown. Knowledge looks privately at up to 3 facedown cards you did not place. Belial looks at one facedown card and may swap it with a card from your hand; it still counts as Offered by the player who placed it. Winnowing discards up to 2 facedown cards unseen.

**Veil.** Counts the Altar, tokens included, at the moment it is revealed.

**Mirage.** Protects every card you Offered or placed on the Altar (not First Fruits, which have no owner) until the Evening: other players cannot Gather, flip, look at, exchange, or destroy it.

**Raphael, Azazel and the Tempest.** Raphael and Azazel destroy only face-up cards, turning your own Offered card face up to do it. The Tempest may destroy a facedown card, unseen; it still cannot touch a card protected by Sanctuary or Mirage.

**Snare.** Pays for each card you Offered or placed with Vessel that another player Gathers, flips, or discards.

**Vessel.** Its card goes on the Altar facedown but is not an Offer. If it has been Gathered or destroyed, or the Day is Neutral, it neither gains nor loses.

**Eternity.** The kept card returns to the Altar, face up, next Morning and counts toward that Day; at the end of that Day it is cleared like any other card.

**Fig Leaves.** In the Evening, before the reveal, you may take one facedown card you Offered this Day back into your hand. It still counts as an Offer for the Miser's Rule.

### **Scoring and the Reaping**

**Nephilim.** A Dark card: it pushes Dark and scores on Dark Days. For any card that asks for a Light card (Twilight, Uriel, Zealot, Seraphim), it also counts as Light.

**Acolyte.** Its color on any Day is the color of the most recent Day won (a Neutral Day does not change it), on the Altar and in your Choir. Before any Day is won, it is Light.

**Heresy, Morning Star and Ordeal.** Decided on your turn, before the reveal, and they last until the end of the Day. With Heresy, your Choir scores its cards of the losing color instead of the winning one. Morning Star costs 1 Glory. Ordeal is discarded when invoked; its doubling still applies that Day, and if your Choir then scores nothing you lose 2 (not on a Neutral Day).

**The Eclipse.** Chosen in the Morning, before First Fruits: the chosen card counts as the other color, in your Choir, for the whole Day.

**The Whisper.** Settled at the Reaping. A Neutral Day is neither right nor wrong.

**Scapegoat.** Used at the Reaping, before anyone gains: pay 2 Glory, and one player whose Choir would score scores nothing.

**The Arbiter.** When your Choir scores, you take 1 of that Glory from a player whose Choir scored less (you choose which). If none of them has Glory, it all comes from the Pot.

**Uriel, Beelzebub, Zealot and Twilight.** They count the other face-up cards in your Choir, not themselves. Nephilim counts as one Light and one Dark.

**Bounty and Prophecy.** Named in the Morning, settled at the end of the Day. A Neutral Day is a miss for Prophecy.

**Hubris.** Counts all Glory the leader gained that Day, from any source.

**Tree of Life and Apple.** They read the Tide track: Tree of Life pays when Light won today and yesterday; Apple when today's winner lost yesterday. A Neutral Day breaks both.

**Firmament and Leviathan.** They read the winning margin: exactly 1 for Firmament, 3 or more for Leviathan.

**Outcast.** Checked after the Choirs score, against everyone's Glory at that moment.

**Gabriel.** You see the hand privately, then name Light or Dark aloud. It pays if any card that player Offers that Day has the named alignment. Tokens are not cards.

**Council.** The named player does not have to agree.

### **Targeting and Protection**

**Targeting** is any ability aimed at another player or their cards. Its six kinds are **Look at, Reveal, Copy, Flip, Replace,** and **Discard**. Allegiance tokens are never Targeted.

**Protection.** Cherubim stops other players from Replacing or Discarding your Choir cards, and from playing cards into your Choir. Tribute makes any player Targeting your Choir pay you 1 Glory first.

**Temptation and the Patron.** Playing a card into another player's Choir is Targeting: Cherubim blocks it and Tribute must be paid. For Temptation, if their Choir is full, they choose which card to discard. The Patron exchanges: you take one of their face-up cards into your hand and put a card from your hand in its place.

**Parasite.** It goes straight into another player's Choir when you Exalt or Gather it. Its holder cannot discard it, not even to make room. At the end of each Day, its planter gains 1 if its holder's Choir scored nothing, and Parasite is discarded if it scored.

**Martyr and Lilith.** Martyr pays for every other card discarded from your Choir, by you or anyone. Lilith may take any card discarded from another player's Choir; if several players hold Lilith, the first in turn order chooses first.

### **Other Cards**

**The Echo.** Copies only *On your turn* abilities, and not spent cards.

**The Oracle.** With only one Event card left in the deck, the Oracle does not look that Morning.

**The Cantor.** Draws after any Offer, a card or a token.

**Seraphim and Legion.** They pay when you Exalt a card of their alignment into your own Choir.

**Forger and Wisdom.** Forger lets you take the Gathered or Exalted card from the discard pile instead. Wisdom's look happens after your Offer (a card or a token) or Exalt.

**Envy.** Usable only while the player with the most Glory has at least 5 more Glory than you.

**Covenant.** Made in the Morning. If either of you Offers that Day, a card or a token, the pact pays nothing.

## **Glossary**

**Activate.** To use a Host ability you choose to use. Always-on abilities are never activated.

**Allegiance token.** A secret Light or Dark token. You hold six at the start. Each scores 1 Glory at the end for every Day its color won. Offered, it counts 2 for its color on the Altar.

**Altar.** The shared pool in the center. Its Light and Dark decide who wins the Day.

**At the end of the Day.** A timing. The ability resolves after the Choirs score at the Reaping.

**Celestial.** The public power each player commands in the Advanced game.

**Choir.** Your row of up to 4 face-up Host cards; you start with 2. Each card of the winning color scores 2 Glory at the Reaping.

**Day.** A round of play: Morning, Noon, and Evening. Every game lasts 7 Days.

**Judgment Day.** Day 7: every player chooses their action in secret before anyone acts; the actions are carried out in turn order, and one that can no longer be done does nothing.

**Exalt.** An action: play a card from your hand into your Choir.

**Facedown.** A card turned face down. On the Altar it still counts; in a Choir it is inert.

**First Fruits.** Each Morning, every player gives one card; they are shuffled and turned face up onto the Altar, so no one knows whose is whose. Not an Offer.

**Gather.** An action: take a card from the Altar into your Choir, or a token into your hidden tokens.

**Glory.** The game's points and its currency. It never drops below 0.

**Miser's Rule.** At the end, whoever Offered the fewest times loses 3 Glory.

**Neutral Day.** A Day no one won: the Altar was tied or empty. No Choir scores, and nothing is marked on the Tide track.

**Offer.** An action: play a card, or an allegiance token, facedown onto the Altar. It earns nothing directly.

**Offering value.** The Light or Dark strength on a Host card.

**Pot.** The shared Glory supply.

**Reaping.** The Evening scoring step: every Choir card of the winning color scores 2 Glory.

**Scored.** A Choir scored if it gained Glory at the Reaping.

**Target.** An ability Targets when it is aimed at another player or their cards.

**Tide track.** The record of which side won each Day. Tokens score from it at the end.

**Upkeep.** Draw back to your hand limit and pass the first player marker.

**Winning margin.** How much heavier the winning side was that Day.
