# The Divide: Quick Guide

*Card data V45, rulebook v15. 3 or 4 players, about 25 to 35 minutes. The full rules are in `The_Divide_Rulebook_v15.md`.*

## The idea in one breath

Everyone secretly holds a mix of **Light** and **Dark** allegiance tokens. Each Day, a shared **Altar** decides which side wins. Your **Choir** scores when its cards' color wins; your **tokens** score at the end for every Day their color won. So every turn you choose between **helping yourself** (building your Choir) and **helping a side** (pushing the Altar), and you never quite know who else is on your side.

## What you need

- **69 Host cards.** Every card is **Light** or **Dark** (+1, or +2 for Hymn and Lamentation). Acolyte takes the color of the most recent Day won.
- **Allegiance tokens:** 9 Light and 9 Dark with 3 players; 12 and 12 with 4.
- **The Tide track:** 7 spaces, and markers that are Light on one side and Dark on the other.
- **Glory** to keep score.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the tokens facedown and deal **6 to each player**. Look at yours and keep them secret. Mixes are uneven: one player may hold 5 Light and 1 Dark, another 3 and 3.
2. Deal everyone **3 cards in hand** and **2 face up in their Choir** (a Choir holds up to 4).
3. Pick a first player.

## A Day (play 7)

**Morning: First Fruits.** Everyone gives one card from hand, facedown. Shuffle them and turn them face up onto the Altar, so **no one knows whose is whose**. Everyone draws 1.

**Noon: one action each**, in turn order. After your action, use any *On your turn* abilities in your Choir.

| Action | What you do | Whom it helps |
|---|---|---|
| **Offer** | Put a card from your hand **facedown** on the Altar, **or one of your tokens** (it counts **2** for its color) | **A side**: everyone who holds that color. And it lets your Choir score tonight (below). |
| **Gather** | Take a card from the Altar into your Choir, or a token into your secret tokens | **You** (and it removes that push from the Altar) |
| **Exalt** | Play a card from your hand into your Choir | **You** |

**Evening.**
1. Shuffle the facedown Offers and tokens together, then turn them face up. **No one learns who Offered what.**
2. Add up Light and Dark. **The heavier side wins the Day.** Mark it on the Tide track. A tie means no one wins.
3. **Choirs score:** each card in your Choir of the winning color gives you **2 Glory**, **but only if you Offered today. No offering, no harvest.**
4. Tokens left on the Altar go, one at a time, to whoever holds the fewest tokens.
5. Clear the Altar and draw back to 3. The first player passes left.

## Day 7: Judgment Day

After First Fruits, **everyone chooses their action in secret**: the action and exactly which card or token. Then the turns go in order and each player carries out what they chose. **If your choice can no longer be done** (someone took that card first, or an Offer is now blocked), **you do nothing.** No one gets to act last with the most information on the Day that decides the game.

## The end

1. **Reveal tokens.** Each token scores **1 Glory for every Day its color won.**
   *Light won 4 Days and Dark won 2; you hold 4 Light and 2 Dark: 4 × 4 + 2 × 2 = 20.*
2. **Most Glory wins.** Ties share the win.

## What to watch for in your first game

- **Build or Offer?** A Day you build instead of Offering, your Choir earns nothing that night. Pick those Days when your Choir's color looks likely to lose anyway.
- **Everyone is partly on your side.** A Light Day pays every Light token at the table. The question is never only "who wins the Day?" but "who paid for it?"
- **A token is your strongest push and your dearest.** Offered, it counts double, but it's gone, and it may end up with a rival.
- **Choirs are public; tokens are not.** A Choir full of Light cards might hide mostly Dark tokens.
- **A one-color Choir** scores big on its Days and nothing on the others. **A mixed Choir** scores a little every Day and tells the table less.
- **Talk.** Deals and promises are allowed and never binding. The Tide track gives everyone the same numbers to argue over: "Light is up 3–1. Whoever holds Light should pay now."

## What changed from the game you knew (V44)

| Before (V44) | Now (V45) |
|---|---|
| Your side was your Choir's public lean | Your side is a **secret mix of tokens** |
| The Reaping: alone +3, pairs +1, three or more 0, losses against the Tide | **2 per Choir card of the winning color**, no losses |
| Offering gave 1 Glory | **Offering gives nothing** directly; it is the public good |
| Race to 12 | **7 Days**, the last a secret-choice **Judgment Day**, then the token reveal |
| 23 Light, 23 Neutral, 23 Dark | **Every card Light or Dark** |
| First Fruits and Offers showed who played them | **Both anonymous** |
| Hidden Roles for 5–6 players | **Retired**: the tokens do its job at 3 and 4 |
| — | **No offering, no harvest**: your Choir scores only on a Day you Offered |

**Advanced game:** add the 9 Celestials (one public power each) and the 17 Events (a new rule each Day). Cards whose text changed for the new scoring are listed in `CHANGELOG.md`.
