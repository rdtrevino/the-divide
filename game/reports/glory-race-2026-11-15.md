# Glory Race: Ending at a Glory Target Instead of After 6 Days

Date: 2026-11-15. V35 Open Game. The race rule tested: **at the end of the Reaping, if any player has reached the target, the game ends** (endgame bonus and tiebreaks as usual). Every player always gets the same number of turns. A safety cap ends the game after Day 12, or after Day 8 in the capped version. The final-Day gift ban is off, because no one knows which Day is last.

Seat fairness uses 12,000 games per player count; the other measures use 3,000 plus the usual skill tests. For reference, the Day leader in V35 has about 2.5 Glory after Day 1, 5.9 after Day 3, and 8.9 after Day 5.

A race fixes the seat problem by itself, because the last Day is no longer tied to a seat in the rotation.

## Takeaway

- **First to 10, or Day 8 at the latest** is the best balance. Seats come out even at both player counts (spread 0.02 to 0.03), the average game stays about 6 Days, skill matters a little more than now, and Celestials are as balanced as V35. About 10% of games reach Day 8 without anyone at 10; then the most Glory wins.
- **The costs.** The leader is the one who ends the game, so last-Day comebacks drop slightly (15% vs 19%) and the Day 3 leader wins a little more often (65% vs 62%). Game length also varies, usually from 4 Days to 8.
- **New rule needed.** With no known final Day, the final-Day gift ban does not work, and a trailing player could hand Glory to someone to end the game. A replacement is needed, for example that gifted Glory does not count toward the target, or that no one may give Glory to a player within 2 of the target.
- Low targets (8) make the game short and snowbally. High targets (15) are fair but drag and often hit the cap.
