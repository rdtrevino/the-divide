# V36: The Race to 10

Date: 2026-11-16. Open Game, 3 and 4 players.

## What changed

- **Ending.** The Open Game no longer lasts a fixed 6 Days. At the end of every Reaping, if any player has 10 or more Glory, the game ends (before the Altar is Refreshed), the endgame bonus is scored, and the most Glory wins. There is no Day limit. Hidden Roles still lasts 6 Days.
- **Gifts.** The final-Day gift ban is gone. Glory may be given at any time, and a gift can end the game.
- **The Arbiter.** "If you lose Glory, give 1 of it to the player, other than you, with the least Glory." (was: a player of your choice)
- **Mammon.** "When you Exalt, pay the Glory cost to the player, other than you, with the least Glory." (was: a player of your choosing)
- **Ties for most or least Glory.** The player using the card chooses among the tied players, unless the card says otherwise (Envy hits every tied leader).
- **Shroud** at game end uses the opposite of the second-to-last Day's Tide.

## Measurements

V35 numbers are the fixed 6-Day game. 3 players, 4 players in brackets. Bots never give Glory as gifts, so these numbers cannot show kingmaking.

| Measure | V35 | V36 |
|---|---|---|
| Average Days | 6 | 6.4 (6.3) |
| Games that end by Day 6 / Day 8 / Day 10 | | 59% / 81% / 93% |
| Real decisions per player | 33.2 (33.4) | 35.4 (35.3) |
| Skill edge (1.00 is chance) | 2.46 (2.95) | 2.81 (3.62) |
| Random player vs smart bots | 0.14 (0.12) | 0.06 (0.05) |
| Day 3 leader goes on to win | 62% (55%) | 64% (57%) |
| Winner was not leading before the last Day | 19% (23%) | 14% (20%) |
| Winning Glory | 12.4 (12.4) | 13.2 (13.3) |
| Celestial spread | 0.12 (0.16) | 0.14 (0.17) |
| Seat spread (12,000 games) | 0.06 (0.20) | 0.04 (0.08) |

The race makes skill count for more, keeps games about as long, and nearly evens out the seats. At 4 players the seat that goes last on Day 1 still has a small edge (1.05 vs 0.97). The costs: fewer last-Day comebacks, and about 1 game in 14 runs past Day 10.

The race rewards steady Glory (Prophecy, Providence, Firmament) and taking Glory from rivals (Leviathan, Envy, Tribute, Mammon), and punishes slow setups (Apple, Morning Star, Eternity). Leviathan is now clearly the strongest card in the game.
