# V38: toward every card between 0.75 and 1.25 (November 16, 2026)

Robert asked for Michael's card to return after the Reaping (or sooner if its owner pays 1 Glory, at any time before the Reaping), and for every card to measure between 0.75 and 1.25. Claude proposed reworks for 17 more cards; Robert accepted 13, kept Veil unchanged, and chose his own versions of Urim, Nephilim and Raguel. This report measures the final set on 60,000 games (3 and 4 players alternating, Open Game, race to 10). Tool: `tools/sim/ab3.ts`. Raw numbers: `v38-all-cards-2026-11-16.json`. Standard report: `sim-2026-11-16-v38-open-3-4p.md`.

The uncertainty is about plus or minus 0.03 for common cards and about 0.05 for rarer ones.
