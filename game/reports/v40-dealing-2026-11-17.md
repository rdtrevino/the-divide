# V40: Morning, Noon and Evening; the Offer reward; the starting Choir

Date: 2026-11-17. Open Game, 3 and 4 players. Raw data: `v40-dealing-2026-11-17.json`.

Robert asked three questions:

1. The turns should not be part of the Morning. Each Day should have three phases (setup, turns, scoring). What should the middle one be called?
2. How many cards should a player start with in their Choir? Should players always draw after First Fruits? Is the "draw 1 card" Offer reward worth keeping?
3. Glory should move between players only when a card says so.

## 1. Morning, Noon, Evening

The middle phase is **Noon**. It keeps the time-of-day pattern Robert likes (Morning, Noon, Evening), it is one word, and it never collides with "Day 1, Day 2". Other names considered: *Midday* and *Daylight* (both contain "day"), *the Labor* and *the Vigil* (break the time-of-day pattern), *Zenith* and *Sext* (less familiar).

With turns at Noon, Morning is exactly the old Unveiling (Event, Declarations, Seed, First Fruits), so "the Unveiling" is retired and Morning is its only name. Six cards said "During the Unveiling"; they now say "In the Morning", which pairs with the existing "In the Evening" cadence. These are wording-only reprints: Prophecy, the Oracle, the Whisper, Creation, Equilibrium and Casting Lots.

## 2. Card flow and the starting Choir

Each line is 6,000 games of equal smart bots, plus 6,000 with one smart bot among random players and 6,000 with one random player among smart bots, on the same seeds. 3 players, with 4 players in brackets.

### The Offer reward

| Measure | V39 | Offer = 1 Glory |
|---|---|---|
| Days per game | 6.21 (6.11) | 6.14 (6.01) |
| Skilled bots choose "draw 1 card" | 3% of Offers | n/a |
| Day 3 leader wins | 65% (58%) | 65% (59%) |
| Celestial spread | 0.127 (0.127) | 0.112 (0.097) |

Skilled players take the card only 3% of the time: the hand is refilled to 3 at Upkeep anyway, so a card drawn at Noon is almost always a card you would have had by morning, bought with a Glory. Removing the choice changes nothing measurable except Vessel, which copied the reward. The skill-edge figure falls (2.83 to 2.69) only because random players can no longer throw Glory away on the draw.

**Vessel** then becomes "place a hand card facedown, gain 1 Glory" every turn, a free second Offer: **1.49**. As "place a hand card facedown, then draw 1 card" (a hidden extra vote that costs no cards) it measures **0.76**, inside the 0.75 to 1.25 target.

### The draw after First Fruits

| Measure | V39 | No draw after First Fruits | No draw, hand of 4 instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days per game | 6.21 (6.11) | 8.63 (8.85) | 6.41 (6.28) |
| Cards in hand at your turn | 3.16 | 2.16 | 3.16 |
| The Dominion | 0.94 (0.95) | **1.40 (1.60)** | 0.94 (0.96) |
| Day 3 leader wins | 65% (58%) | 58% (49%) | 68% (60%) |
| Celestial spread | 0.127 (0.127) | 0.192 (0.243) | 0.106 (0.094) |

(The last two columns also have the Offer at 1 Glory.)

Without the draw, hands shrink to 2, Offers fall from 64% of actions to 54%, games stretch to almost 9 Days, and a 4-card hand (the Dominion) becomes dominant. Players need that card. A hand of 4 with no draw supplies it too, but it slightly increases snowballing, widens the card spread (Urim 0.65, Vessel 1.59, Heresy 1.25), and needs a new Dominion card. **The draw after First Fruits stays.**

### The starting Choir

All with the Offer at 1 Glory and the new Vessel.

| Starting Choir | Days | Day 3 leader wins | Day 1 Reaping swing | Celestial spread | Core decisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 random (V39) | 6.25 (6.13) | 65% (59%) | 1.07 | 0.112 (0.101) | 29.7 |
| 2 random | 6.48 (6.35) | 62% (56%) | 0.85 | 0.115 (0.142) | 29.3 |
| 1 random* | 7.00 (6.79) | 60% (56%) | 0.69 | 0.168 (0.174) | 34.6 |
| 0* | 7.58 (7.34) | 60% (56%) | 0.40 | 0.197 (0.237) | 35.2 |
| **Choose 2 of 5** | 6.66 (6.52) | **63% (57%)** | 1.08 | **0.100** (0.114) | 31.3 |
| Choose 3 of 6 | 6.57 (6.40) | 63% (57%) | 1.10 | 0.103 (0.125) | 32.3 |

(The 2-random row was run with the earlier Vessel, one card in 66. *The 1-random and 0 rows were run with the V39 Offer reward, so their decision counts include the reward choice; compare their other columns.)

- **Fewer random cards** shrink the early-luck swing, but below 2 the Celestials drift out of balance and games run long, because a Choir of 1 barely scores.
- **Choosing** removes the luck of the deal altogether and gives every player a real first decision. Choose 2 and choose 3 measure almost the same.
- **Choose 2 of 5** wins on design: the first decision is small (2 of 5 rather than 3 of 6), the Choir has an open third place, so the first Gather or Exalt adds a card without a discard, and the deal mirrors First Fruits (everyone chooses in secret and reveals together). Choose 3 pushes Heresy to 1.34.

### Every card under the new rules (30,000 bot games, 3 and 4 players alternating)

Spread of card win ratios: 0.108 (V39) to 0.109. Outside 0.75 to 1.25: **Free Will 1.26** (was 1.17) and **Heresy 1.27** (was 1.16), both just over and worth watching. Urim (0.71 to 0.82), Nephilim (0.73 to 0.81) and Veil (0.75 to 0.86) all move back inside the range, because players now choose their Choir. Largest other moves: Vessel 1.15 to 0.76, Raguel 0.86 to 1.00, Firmament 1.04 to 1.18, Jubilee 0.89 to 1.02.

## 3. Glory only by card

Removed from the rules, the engine and the site: the gift rule, its Hidden Roles line, "transferable" in the glossary, and "Glory transfers" on Babel (wording-only reprint: "No table talk or deals this Day."). Card abilities that move Glory (Mammon, the Arbiter, Council, Covenant, Raguel, Hubris, Tribute) are unchanged. Games recorded with a gift no longer replay move by move; the Replay page already shows their recorded scores.

## Adopted in V40

- Each Day is Morning, Noon, Evening. "During the Unveiling" becomes "In the Morning" on six cards (wording).
- Setup: deal 5 Host cards (the Dominion 6); everyone chooses 2 for their starting Choir and reveals them together.
- The Offer reward is 1 Glory.
- First Fruits keeps its draw.
- Vessel: "On your turn: You may place a card from your hand on the Altar facedown, then draw 1 card. This is not an Offer." (rules change; reprint)
- Babel: "No table talk or deals this Day." (wording)
- Glory moves between players only through card abilities.
