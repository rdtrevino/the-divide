# V37 balance changes (November 16, 2026)

Nine Host cards changed to pull the five strongest and five weakest V36 cards toward a win ratio of 1.00. Michael, the sixth candidate, is still open (see below). Each option was measured on the same 20,000 seeds (3 and 4 players alternating, Open Game, race to 10). The full list of options tried is in `v37-candidates-2026-11-16.md`; the full V37 card table is in `sim-2026-11-16-v37-open-3-4p.md`.

## Adopted

| Card | V37 text | V36 | V37 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leviathan | At the Reaping: If your Alignment did not gain you Glory, gain 1 Glory if any other player loses Glory this Reaping. | 1.44 | 1.11 |
| Tree of Life | On your turn: Take the top card of the discard pile into your hand, then discard a card from your hand. | 1.36 | 1.09 |
| Envy | On your turn: If the player with the most Glory has at least 3 more Glory than you, they lose 1 Glory. Each tied leader loses 1. | 1.30 | 1.08 |
| Tribute | Other players must pay 1 Glory to the Pot before they may Target any card in your Choir with an ability. | 1.29 | 1.05 |
| Watchers | On your turn: Look at the top 3 cards of the Host deck. Put 1 into your hand and discard a card from your hand, then return the rest in any order. | 0.66 | 0.94 |
| Forger | When you Gather, you may take any Host card from the discard pile instead of a card from the Altar or the deck. | 0.67 | 0.87 |
| Apple | At the Reaping: If your Choir's Alignment is the opposite color from the previous Day's Tide, gain 1 Glory. | 0.67 | 0.99 |
| Council | On your turn: You may replace another card in your Choir with a card from your hand. Then each other player may do the same by paying you 1 Glory. Replaced cards are discarded. | 0.67 | 0.88 |
| Mirage | Your facedown Offerings cannot be flipped, looked at, exchanged, or destroyed before the Evening. When the Altar is revealed, if any of your facedown Offerings is still on it, draw 1 card. | 0.69 | 0.73 |

Mirage is still weak. It is a bluffing card and the bots cannot bluff, so its real strength needs a human playtest.

## Game quality (V37, 6,000 games per test; 4 players in brackets)

Skill edge 2.81 (3.59), unchanged from V36 (2.81, 3.62). The sole leader after Day 3 wins 64% (56%). Average length 6.4 Days. Card win ratio spread (standard deviation) fell from 0.190 to about 0.16.

## Michael (open)

V36 text: "On your turn: Flip a Host card in a player's Choir Facedown. That player may pay 1 Glory on their turn to Flip it face up." With the other V37 changes in place, Michael is the strongest card at **1.47**.

| Michael option | Engine variant | Win ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Unchanged | (none) | 1.47 |
| The card flips face up at the start of the next Day, or its owner may pay 1 Glory on their turn to flip it sooner | `mic-day` | 1.30 |
| Its owner may flip it face up on their turn for free | `mic-free` | 1.20 |
| Both of the above | `mic-day+mic-free` | 1.18 |
